The week in brief

  • Week 43 (19 to 23 October): 35 findings and 16 library documents across five editions, and the second Friday check of 15 directory listings.
  • Saudi calendar: after the 10 Oct attack, the Saudi Retail Forum moved to March 2027, FII10 was postponed to 2027 and Intersec Saudi Arabia moved to October 2027; King Khalid International reopened on 17 Oct, but several airlines have suspended Saudi flights into November and beyond, a point to plan around for RCM Jeddah on 7 to 8 Dec.
  • Pipeline: Arada launched Arada KSA with a SAR50bn land bank and a SAR14bn Riyadh master plan, Talaat Moustafa Group agreed four mixed-use mega-projects with the Egyptian state worth about EGP 11 trillion, Awqaf Dubai is building an AED73m endowment shopping centre in Hind City, and Arada's own figures put Madar Mall at Aljada at 1.1m sq ft GLA for December 2028.
  • Openings and tenants: Primark opened its first Qatar store at Doha Festival City on 21 Oct, eight days early; Malabar opened its 13th Qatar showroom at Safari Mall; Majid Al Futtaim marked 25 years of City Centre Muscat and five of Mall of Oman; One Retail will bring Primor to Morocco in 2027; and Dubai Shopping Festival runs from 4 Dec to 17 Jan.
  • Members: Majid Al Futtaim's Precision Media launched Precision Insights to link ads to sales, Aldar and ADIB launched Darna cards worth up to 10% back at Aldar malls, and Cenomi won dismissal of the Mall of Dhahran owner's claims, subject to appeal.
  • AI shopping agents split the market: Amazon and eBay block them, while American Express published a framework for agent purchases, Salesforce agreed to buy Braze for about $5.66bn and Walmart opened an Agent Gateway; in the UAE, Emirates NBD will run OpenAI models in-country, Abu Dhabi set up TAMM Global and tourist VAT refunds now take one business day.
  • Türkiye: Torunlar GYO set Forum Kayseri AVM's final price at EUR 131,904,579, and WinsTown AVM in Diyarbakır handed its leasing to MSP.
  • 2027 Directory: the week sent Forum Kayseri AVM (Add now), Madar Mall (Add to pipeline) and the Hind City centre (Check first) to sheet 10, and Friday's check found five listing changes and seven 2022-era pipeline listings that could not be confirmed from owners' sources.
