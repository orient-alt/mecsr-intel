Visit Qatar's "Hala Summer" turns Doha malls into family-activity hubs through September

DestinationsQatar27 Jul 2026 · recent

Qatar Tribune reports Visit Qatar has partnered with Doha Mall and other retail destinations on a three-month "Hala Summer" campaign (July–September 2026), featuring a dedicated family activity zone, roaming parades, interactive games with shopping-voucher prizes, and a dedicated Hala Summer Shopping Festival running 1–31 August. MEMBER NEWS SAUDI ARABIA Qiddiya awards viewing-lounge contract at Speed Park New Project 03 Jul 2026

MembersSaudi ArabiaNew property3 Jul 2026 · recentMECSR member

Company: Qiddiya Investment Company (Priority 1 member, Riyadh). QIC has awarded El-Seif Engineering Contracting a contract to build a four-storey Exclusive Viewing Lounge within the Speed Park development at Qiddiya Entertainment City, near Riyadh. The building will provide race-day spectator areas, indoor lounges, guest hospitality amenities and service spaces supporting major motorsport events (Speed Park is anchored by Saudi Arabia's new F1-standard circuit). Ammico Contracting had already completed enabling works ahead of the main build. While primarily an entertainment/motorsport facility rather than a retail-specific structure, it is directory-relevant as part of Qiddiya's broader mixed-use entertainment-city pipeline, which includes retail and F&B components under the same masterplan. Distinct from Qiddiya's Six Flags/Aquarabia theme-park openings and high-speed-rail PPP news; not previously carried in this series.

MEMBER NEWS UAE Emaar tops Dubai developer sales-value rankings for 2026 Financial 23 Jul 2026

MembersUAEFinancial23 Jul 2026 · recentMECSR member

Company: Emaar Properties / Emaar Development (Priority 1 member, Dubai). A market analysis from fäm Properties, based on DXBinteract transaction data as of 22 July 2026, shows Emaar recorded the highest value of Dubai residential sales transactions year-to-date at AED30.6 billion, 83.2% ahead of DAMAC in second place (AED16.7bn). Emaar also led the luxury segment (properties above AED15 million) with 387 transactions worth AED8.4 billion, ahead of Omniyat, and topped the market in delivered projects (9) and units (3,819) and in active projects under construction (150). Azizi led on overall transaction volume, driven by the affordable (sub-AED2m) segment. While a residential-market data point rather than a retail-specific announcement, the ranking is directory-relevant given Emaar's parallel mall and community-retail portfolio, which typically tracks residential absorption across its master-planned communities. This is distinct from Emaar's AED200bn Dubai masterplan (first reported 3 Jul) and the Emaar–ADCB mortgage partnership (reported 29 Jul), today's item is the fäm Properties developer-ranking report specifically, not previously carried in this series.

UAE firms step up AI investment, but engagement maturity lags customer expectations

AI & techUAEMarket dataJul 2026 · recent

Khaleej Times reports that 76% of UAE businesses plan to invest in AI-powered customer engagement in 2026, with an equal share investing in omnichannel technologies and 74% saying AI will be critical for acquiring and retaining customers. Despite the spend, only 15% of UAE marketers achieved a high engagement-maturity score; more than half of surveyed enterprises said they cannot access and use real-time data, and 64% report "dark data" collected but never used. The piece frames a widening gap between board-level AI investment expectations and the operational data work required to convert that spend into seamless CX, a useful data point for MECSC members benchmarking their own AI/CX maturity.
