EGYPT Majid Al Futtaim breaks ground on JUNCTION business & lifestyle park, West Cairo New Property / Mixed-Use 01 Jun 2026

DestinationsEgyptNew property1 Jun 2026 · recent

Majid Al Futtaim officially broke ground on JUNCTION, its next-generation business park in West Cairo, marking the commencement of an EGP 500 million enabling-works phase, the first foundational step within total planned investment exceeding EGP 20 billion ($385m). Located directly adjacent to Mall of Egypt with connections to Al Wahat Road, the Cairo–Alex Desert Road and the Ring Road, JUNCTION spans approximately 99,000 sqm of built-up area and will bring together eight office buildings (440 modular units), a five-star hotel operated by 25hours Hotels by Ennismore, and more than 50 curated retail and F&B outlets, making it a genuine mixed-use retail addition to the Egyptian market rather than a pure office scheme. The project targets LEED Gold certification. Not previously captured in this report series (distinct from the Dubai Development "Walk'n" West Cairo project reported 6 Jul).

SAUDI ARABIA Al Othaim Park (Dammam) passes 64% completion Status Update 14 Jun 2026

DestinationsSaudi ArabiaStatus update14 Jun 2026 · recent

Al Othaim Park, a SAR 2.34 billion mixed-use destination in Dammam developed by Al Othaim Investment, has surpassed 64% construction completion and remains on track for a 2027 opening. The 225,000 sqm site (939,000 sqm total built-up area) sits on King Fahd Road linking King Fahd International Airport to central Dammam, and will comprise a regional shopping mall (138,000 sqm leasable), 950 residential units, a 180-key hotel plus 70 hotel apartments, and 23,000 sqm of offices, with an expected 10 million annual visitors on completion. This updates, with fresh, dated completion data, a broader Al Othaim Eastern Province pipeline (also covering Al Othaim Park Jubail and Al Othaim Circle Khobar) previously flagged in the 28 Jul report via a Sep 2025 RLI piece that was excluded there as too old; this Maaal article is a distinct, newer data point on the Dammam asset specifically.

MEMBER NEWS SAUDI ARABIA Knowledge Economic City signs Letter of Intent with BSF Capital for SAR 7.5bn Knowledge Gardens fund New Project 28 Jul 2026

MembersSaudi ArabiaNew property28 Jul 2026 · in windowMECSR member

Company: Knowledge Economic City (Priority 1 member, Madinah, Saudi Arabia), together with subsidiary Algharraa International Real Estate Development Company. KEC and Algharraa signed a Letter of Intent with BSF Capital to establish and manage a closed-ended private real estate investment fund to develop Knowledge Gardens, planned as one of the largest mixed-use developments within Knowledge Economic City, sited adjacent to the Haramain High-Speed Railway Station in Madinah. The scheme carries an estimated total project cost of ~SAR 7.5 billion ($2bn) and projected revenues of ~SAR 10.2 billion, across a 332,054 sqm net land area with 897,542 sqm of Gross Floor Area, comprising 4,198 residential units, 183 serviced apartments, 450 hotel keys, ~80,000 sqm of commercial and office GFA , plus a community centre, mosque, kindergarten and 52,000+ sqm of parks. Fourmen Investment Company is named as lead developer/investor and BSF Capital as proposed fund manager; KEC/Algharraa are expected to contribute land valued at ~SAR 1.66bn for ~39.9% of fund equity. The LOI runs 120 days for definitive agreements and regulatory approvals. This is the fourth Knowledge Economic City financing/land story captured by this series in July alone (SAIB financing 6 Jul, Madinah land sale 19 Jul, SALCO infrastructure award 27 Jul), a Priority 1 member showing sustained, dated deal-flow.
