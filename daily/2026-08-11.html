Member News Deal / JV KEC signs Apparel Group as premier retail partner at Multaqa AlMadinah Mall: 24 brands, 10 Madinah debuts

MembersSaudi ArabiaDeal / JV9 Aug 2026 · in windowMECSR member

Member Knowledge Economic City has entered a strategic partnership with UAE-based Apparel Group to bring 24 international retail and dining brands across 8,686 m² to Multaqa AlMadinah Mall, the retail heart of KEC's mixed-use destination inside the Haram Boundary (which also includes a 390-key Hilton hotel and 66 Hilton-branded residences). Abu Dhabi launches free tourist visas for Indian travellers: a friction-removal play in the visitor journey

CXUAECX10 Aug 2026 · in window

The Department of Culture and Tourism (DCT) Abu Dhabi will cover the UAE entry-visa cost (approx. $77 per traveller) for Indian passport holders who book a return flight from India plus at least three nights in an Abu Dhabi hotel through participating travel partners. The pilot runs 1 August – 31 October 2026 with capacity for 20,000 visas (~$1.5m in direct subsidy), processed via appointed Destination Management Companies rather than individual applications. For CX professionals, it is a clear example of a government removing a cost/effort barrier at the very start of the visitor experience to compete for a priority source market.

Global: Strada launches multi-channel AI claims intake across voice, chat, email and SMS (Recent Background)

AI & techGlobalCX4 Aug 2026 · recent

Insurance-focused AI firm Strada now automates the full first-notice-of-loss (FNOL) cycle in one workflow, a policyholder can report by phone, receive SMS confirmations and email documents, with structured claim data written straight into carrier systems, replacing manual entry. Most AI service tools still handle one channel; unifying voice + digital in a single automated intake is a marker of where omnichannel service automation is heading for GCC insurers, banks and telecoms.
