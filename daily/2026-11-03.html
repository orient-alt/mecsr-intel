Al Khobar Downtown launched: an 829,000 sqm waterfront scheme with more than 139,000 sqm of retail and dining

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · Al KhobarPipeline2 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Add to pipeline

Saudi Downtown Company (SDC), owned by the Public Investment Fund, launched Al Khobar Downtown on 1 November in the presence of the Eastern Province Governor. The 829,000 sqm waterfront scheme is to include more than 139,000 sqm of retail, restaurant and cafe space, more than 4,000 homes, more than 500 hotel rooms and 40,000 sqm of offices; Arab News and Construction Week Saudi agree on these figures. Its mall is the one Cenomi Centers is developing with SDC, under the design-and-build contract it awarded to Lynx Contracting in June, and SDC also signed three agreements with Sumou Real Estate for residential buildings. No completion date or total investment was given.

Why it matters: It gives the first master-plan figures for an Eastern Province destination already on the 2027 Directory pipeline list, and SDC is applying the same Downtown model in 12 Saudi cities.

M&S to open its largest Foodhall outside the UK, 15,327 sq ft, at Dubai Festival City in December

DestinationsUAE · DubaiTenant news2 Nov 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Update listing · Low

Marks & Spencer and its long-term partner Al-Futtaim will open a 15,327 sq ft Foodhall at Dubai Festival City in December 2026, M&S's largest Foodhall outside the UK and the first of five large-format UAE Foodhalls planned over the next 12 months; the other four sites were not named. The store will carry more than 3,500 M&S products and about 500 locally sourced lines and add a Fruit Butcher and a Sushi Counter, which M&S calls a first for the Middle East. Al-Futtaim has run an M&S fashion store at Dubai Festival City since 2018.

Why it matters: A sizeable food store with fresh-food counters arrives at a member's mall, and four more UAE Foodhall sites will need placing in the Directory once named.

Magrabi Retail agrees to buy Dubai Optical and take its 15 Dubai Optical-branded UAE stores

MembersUAE · DubaiDeal / JV2 Nov 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: High

Magrabi Retail has agreed to acquire Dubai Optical, a UAE optical retailer founded in 1978, for an undisclosed price. Dubai Optical runs 19 stores under the Dubai Optical and Optivision brands in Dubai, Sharjah, Fujairah and Ajman; the deal covers 100% of the company, but Magrabi takes only the 15 Dubai Optical-branded stores, and the announcement does not say what happens to the Optivision stores. Completion needs merger-control clearance from the UAE Ministry of Economy. Khaleej Times calls it Magrabi's fourth major acquisition in two years; its majority stake in Egypt's Baraka Optics still awaits competition clearance.

Why it matters: A member is consolidating UAE optical retail, and on completion 15 more UAE stores move into its portfolio, which matters for tenant data and member relations.

UAE launches an Annual Government Challenge for federal teams, first focused on assistive AI

CXUAELaunch2 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid, Vice President and Ruler of Dubai, launched an Annual Government Challenge for UAE Government employees and teams at the start of UAE Innovation Month, The National reported on 2 November. The first edition focuses on assistive artificial intelligence, and the nine leading projects or teams will be recognised for their impact on services, operations and government projects. It follows the federal plan to move half of government services to AI agents within two years.

Why it matters: A yearly federal showcase of AI-assisted service improvement gives MECSC benchmarks and possible speakers; it is not the government services transformation promised for November.

Dubai launches the invitation-only Dubai Access Club for repeat visitors

CXUAE · DubaiLaunch1 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed, Crown Prince of Dubai, launched the Dubai Access Club on 1 November, an invitation-only membership programme for repeat visitors that Dubai's government calls the world's first city-led tourism membership. It was developed by the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs, the Department of Economy and Tourism and Digital Dubai, and single- and multiple-entry tourist visa applications are being added to the Visit Dubai website, with GDRFA still issuing the visas. Member benefits and partners have not been disclosed.

Why it matters: A city-run membership for repeat visitors could become a channel for mall and retailer offers once its benefits and partners are named, with the Dubai Shopping Festival opening on 4 December.

Abu Dhabi signs OpenAI agreements; a machine-readable government pilot is live for food-import checks

AI & techUAE · Abu DhabiDeal / JV2 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Abu Dhabi signed a set of agreements with OpenAI after Crown Prince Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed met OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman, The National reported on 2 November. Under the Machine-Readable Government initiative, led by the Department of Government Enablement, selected policies and regulations are turned into machine-readable form so that AI can help officials with routine compliance checks for permits and certificates, with officials keeping the final decision. The first pilot is in production with the Abu Dhabi Agriculture and Food Safety Authority for food imports. Other agreements cover investment analysis with Mubadala and a wider partnership with Inception42; no financial terms were given.

Why it matters: The live pilot touches grocery and F&B importers directly, and if it spreads to other permits it could shorten licensing for mall tenants in Abu Dhabi.
