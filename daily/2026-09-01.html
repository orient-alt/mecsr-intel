Koru Florya AVM opens in Istanbul

DestinationsTürkiyeNew property31 Aug 2026 · in window

Koru Florya AVM, a new three-level shopping and lifestyle centre in the Florya district of Istanbul, opened its doors on 30 August 2026. The scheme spans Street, Podium and Koru levels linked by a single walking corridor beneath a glass-domed atrium, with landscaped outdoor areas. More than 220 brands are represented across fashion, cosmetics, technology, home goods and food service, mixing established retailers with concepts new to the area, reported tenants include Zara, MediaMarkt, Watsons, the LEGO Store, Shake Shack and Popeyes. Access is positioned around the E5 highway, a direct metrobüs stop at Cennet Mahallesi, and a free shuttle from Florya Marmaray station roughly 1.4 km away. Developer/owner and total GLA were not disclosed in opening-day coverage, flagged for the directory action list below.

Zawaya & EDIC unveil EGP 3.5bn SIA Boulevard, West Cairo

DestinationsEgyptNew property16 Aug 2026 · recent

Zawaya Developments and EDIC Developments have announced a strategic partnership to build "SIA Boulevard," an EGP 3.5bn mixed-use retail scheme at Al-Najda Square in 6th of October City, West Cairo. The open-air boulevard will carry retail units across ground and first floors alongside medical clinics, flexible-configuration offices, hospitality units, a sports zone (operated by HPadel & CrossFit) and a children's entertainment area, with landscaping and water features. Excavation and construction are under way, with the developers targeting operational status within roughly three years. Asset Pillar Development has been appointed for property management.

Idle Ersu factory in Konya to become mall-anchored mixed-use scheme

DestinationsTürkiyeNew property28 Aug 2026 · recent

Listed food producer Ersu Meyve ve Gıda Sanayi A.Ş. is converting its disused factory site in Ereğli, Konya into a roughly 110,000 sqm mixed-use destination combining a shopping mall (AVM), a traditional bazaar (bedesten), a hotel, loft apartments and a technology centre. Konya Metropolitan Municipality has approved "Trade-Tourism Zone" rezoning, and architectural design work has begun with an appointed design firm. No construction start date or opening timeline has been published yet, this is an early-stage announcement, not a groundbreaking.

Turkish mall revenue grows on paper, but inflation erases the gain

DestinationsTürkiyeMarket data27 Aug 2026 · recent

The Shopping Centers and Investors Association (AYD), with Akademetre Research, reported that Turkey's mall productivity index rose 25.2% year-on-year in July 2026 to 5,169 points. Adjusted for the period's 31.75% annual consumer inflation, the same figure represents an approximately 5% real-terms contraction, evidence that footfall and nominal spending are holding up but purchasing power at Turkish malls continues to erode. Istanbul malls are reported to generate roughly 27% more revenue per square metre than those in Anatolia.

New Murabba signs SAR 1bn hospital JV with Almana Medical Group

MembersSaudi ArabiaNew property26 Aug 2026 · recentMECSR member

New Murabba Development Company (member) has partnered with Almana Medical Group on a roughly SAR 1bn (US$267m) general hospital inside New Murabba's "Modern Downtown" district in Riyadh. The 200-bed facility will offer emergency care, family medicine, paediatrics, cardiology, neurosciences, orthopaedics and neonatal care. Acting CEO Eng. Sabah Barakat described it as "the first of many" ecosystem partnerships as the giga-project, designed around a 15-minute-community model for more than 280,000 planned residents, moves from planning into execution. Not itself a retail asset, but a concrete signal of Modern Downtown's shift toward delivery, relevant to the mixed-use retail programme MECSR members are tracking around the project.

Diriyah Company secures SAR 2bn financing for branded residences

MembersSaudi ArabiaFinancial18 Aug 2026 · recentMECSR member

Diriyah Company (member) has secured a SAR 2bn (US$533m) financing facility from Arab National Bank to fund construction of more than 300 branded residences at Wadi Safar, part of an 18,000-home residential strategy across the PIF-backed Diriyah giga-project. Group CEO Jerry Inzerillo confirmed the residences remain "the flagship of our larger residential strategy," with Diriyah targeting 6,500 hotel rooms open ahead of Expo 2030 and more than SAR 110bn in contracts already awarded. Residential rather than retail-specific, but logged as material corporate financing news for a Priority 1 member.

Bahrain's Municipalities Ministry consolidates customer service into a four-centre network

CXBahrainCX26 Aug 2026 · recent

Bahrain's Ministry of Municipalities Affairs and Agriculture has reorganised its customer-facing footprint into four physical service centres, in Muharraq, A'ali, Hamad Town and Riffa, plus a virtual centre reachable on 17989898. Under-Secretary Shaikh Mohammed bin Ahmed Al Khalifa framed the move explicitly around customer experience, stating the "reorganisation aims to improve access to municipal services, enhance customer experience and increase operational efficiency." From 1 September, Capital Municipality customers can transact at any of the four locations, replacing the previous standalone Capital Municipality centre, with digital channels layered in so residents and investors can complete many transactions remotely. For MECSC members, this is a straightforward but useful data point: a government body distributing customer volume across a wider physical network, paired with a virtual option, rather than centralising service in one location, worth watching for early citizen feedback once the new network goes live.

Etihad Rail confirms 30 September passenger-service launch to Dubai

CXUAECX4 Aug 2026 · recent

Etihad Rail has confirmed staged passenger openings across the UAE: Dubai and Al Dhaid stations from 30 September 2026, followed by Liwa and Madinat Zayed (30 November), Al Dhafra (30 December) and Sharjah (30 March 2027). Ahead of the Dubai launch, the operator reports passengers reserving trips roughly two weeks in advance, a sign of deliberate travel planning rather than casual trial, and a diverse early customer base spanning families, business travellers, schools, leisure travellers and People of Determination. More than 70,000 tickets have already been sold on the Abu Dhabi–Fujairah route. The operator's own framing, "safety, reliability and customer experience" as it scales capacity, makes this a useful example for MECSC members of a major infrastructure rollout treating booking behaviour and passenger-segment diversity as customer-experience signals worth publishing, not just operational statistics.

LEAP 2026, Saudi Arabia's flagship AI and technology conference, now in its fifth edition, opened in Riyadh on 31 August and runs through 3 September. Concentrix commits $1bn to Egypt's AI-enabled CX/BPO sector

AI & techEgyptMarket data28 Aug 2026 · recent

Concentrix, a US-listed customer-experience and technology-services provider, is investing $1bn to grow its Egypt workforce from roughly 24,500 to 35,000 employees by 2028 across 13 operating centres, with about 98.8% of the Egyptian business's revenue export-related. The expansion pairs continued headcount growth with the integration of "generative AI, automation and other digital technologies" into service delivery, positioned as a shift toward higher-value, technology-enabled contact-centre operations rather than pure labour-cost competition. It lands alongside a separate national data point: Egypt's broader offshoring sector generated $5.2bn in revenue this year, with AI and digital services credited as the main growth driver. The practical read for MECSC members: this is a rare Gulf-region data point running counter to the "AI cuts contact-centre jobs" narrative logged elsewhere in this series (British Gas, Uber, Microsoft), Egypt's CX/BPO sector is scaling headcount and AI adoption simultaneously, which is worth tracking as a contrasting model for AI-augmented (rather than AI-replaced) customer service delivery.
