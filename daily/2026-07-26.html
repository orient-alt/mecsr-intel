BAHRAIN Seef Properties appoints Cinqo Contracting for new Seef Mall construction package Status Update 21 Jul 2026

DestinationsBahrainStatus update21 Jul 2026 · recent

Seef Properties signed an agreement with Cinqo Contracting to execute a further package of works within the ongoing Seef Mall – Seef District redevelopment, following June's award to Mohammed Jalal Contracting for the main construction works. Cinqo will build a multi-storey car park on the mall's southern side plus new external elevations and main gates, increasing capacity and improving accessibility ahead of the mall's architectural repositioning. EGYPT Gulf Egypt unveils EGP20bn ($401m) Sharm El Sheikh mixed-use tourism destination New Property / Mixed-Use 13 Jul 2026

DestinationsEgyptNew property13 Jul 2026 · recent

Gulf Egypt for Hotels and Tourism (a subsidiary of United Real Estate Company) unveiled plans for a 354,458 sqm mixed-use tourism and hospitality development in Sharm El Sheikh with roughly 750 metres of beachfront, near Sharm El Sheikh International Airport. The project, announced as the company marks 50 years in Egypt, combines a luxury hotel, two branded residential communities, an internationally branded beach club, and retail, leisure and wellness facilities. Gulf Egypt signed a cooperation protocol with Al Montazah Tourism & Investment Company (land acquisition) and advisory agreements with HVS and JLL. UAE Aldar unveils Marsa Al Saadiyat: AED100bn ($27.2bn) waterfront masterplan New Property / Mixed-Use 22 Jul 2026

DestinationsUAENew property22 Jul 2026 · recent

Aldar launched Marsa Al Saadiyat, the final phase of the Saadiyat Island masterplan in Abu Dhabi, inaugurated by Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi. The waterfront district will house private mansions, branded residences, luxury villas and waterfront apartments for more than 58,000 residents, alongside retail, dining and leisure components. Site enabling and infrastructure works are expected to begin in Q3 2026, with the first phase of home sales starting in H2 2026. This is a distinct, larger project from Aldar's Yas Point announcement covered in the 13 Jul report, both add to Aldar's active retail-adjacent development pipeline on Abu Dhabi's islands.

SAP Engagement Index: UAE firms accelerate AI investment, but engagement maturity lags

AI & techUAEMarket data19 Jul 2026 · recent
