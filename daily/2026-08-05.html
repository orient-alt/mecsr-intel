Dubai Government Human Resources Department launches "Government Chief Talent Officer Programme" (G-CTO)

CXUAELeadership31 Jul 2026 · recent

DGHR has launched the G-CTO programme to develop government talent leaders and equip them to drive workforce transformation across Dubai government entities, with a particular focus on readiness for AI-era work. The first cohort features 30 participants from 21 government entities and runs over eight months, combining specialised learning sessions, applied projects and executive coaching. While framed as an HR/talent initiative, the programme is directly relevant to CX leadership pipelines, since it is explicitly designed to prepare government leaders to navigate AI-driven workforce and service transformation, a precursor to the citizen-facing service improvements MECSC members track.

Dubai Municipality launches "Account Manager" initiative for strategic customers

CXUAECX23 Jul 2026 · recent

Dubai Municipality has introduced a dedicated Account Manager model for key customer segments, individuals, food traders, real estate developers, contractors and engineering consultants, moving away from a traditional transactional service model towards proactive, relationship-based support. Account Managers are tasked with responding to enquiries and following up on transactions within one working day, coordinating directly across departments, and monitoring interactions through smart systems with monthly reporting for continuous improvement. Manal Bin Yaroof, director of the Customer Happiness Department, framed the initiative as reinforcing Dubai's positioning as a global city built around customer-centred government services.

MOHRE adds Tabby instalment option for ministry fees and fines

CXUAECX22 Jul 2026 · recent

The UAE's Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation has launched a buy-now-pay-later payment option via Tabby, letting individuals and businesses split ministry service fee payments into monthly instalments. AethexAI launches with $3m pre-seed to build enterprise voice AI for Africa and the Middle East

AI & techMENAMarket dataJun 2026 · recent

AethexAI emerged from stealth with a $3 million pre-seed round, led by 4DX Ventures with participation from Enza Capital, Dorm Room Fund, Mojo Ventures and 26 Fund, to scale its enterprise voice AI platform across Africa and the Middle East. The platform is powered by Kora 1, a proprietary family of voice models trained on licensed datasets from call centres, radio networks and content platforms, positioning the company squarely in the contact-centre and CX voice-AI space that MECSC members are evaluating for bilingual (Arabic/English) deployments. Founders Mariama Diallo and Ayooluwa Odemuyiwa cited performance gaps in existing voice AI as the founding motivation.

Inception42 (G42) targets Q3 2026 for national agentic AI rollout across UAE government

AI & techUAEAI / tech7 Jul 2026 · recent

Abu Dhabi's Inception42, the sovereign agentic AI company owned by G42, plans to begin deploying agentic AI systems across UAE federal government administration by Q3 2026, with CEO Ashish Koshy targeting automation of 50% of federal government operations within two years. Built on the Catalyst platform in partnership with Microsoft, the deployment lets AI agents run inside familiar tools such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, with a "command centre" for human oversight of agent performance. While government-operations-focused rather than purely CX, this is directly relevant to MECSC members tracking the pace and governance model of large-scale agentic AI adoption that will eventually touch citizen and resident-facing services region-wide.
