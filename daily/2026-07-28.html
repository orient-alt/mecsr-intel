QATAR Visit Qatar launches Hala Summer Shopping Festival across 11 malls Market 27 Jul 2026

DestinationsQatarMarket data27 Jul 2026 · in window

As part of the wider Hala Summer campaign (running to end of September), Visit Qatar unveiled the Hala Summer Shopping Festival, a month-long shopping and dining activation from 1–31 August 2026. The festival unites Qatar's leading shopping malls, Place Vendôme, Doha Festival City, Mall of Qatar, Villaggio, City Center, Landmark, Doha Mall, West Walk, Gewan, Hyatt Plaza and Al Hazm, with restaurants from the Taste of Qatar network, offering exclusive promotions, set menus and discounts. Shoppers spending a minimum of QR200 can redeem stamped receipts for raffle coupons; four weekly draws will be hosted at Doha Festival City, Mall of Qatar, Place Vendôme and Villaggio, with prizes including five cars. The activation gives all eleven participating destinations a coordinated footfall driver through August, directly relevant to MECSR's Qatar-listed directory properties.

NAS Gardens Community Mall confirmed for August 2026 opening

DestinationsUAEdate unverified

Meraas developer Shamal confirmed NAS Gardens Community Mall, inside the Nad Al Sheba Gardens villa community (about 10 minutes from Downtown Dubai), will open in August 2026. Waitrose (under Spinneys' UAE licence) anchors as supermarket, alongside a nursery, health and fitness facilities, cafés and restaurants, and two local F&B concepts (Kokoro Handroll Bar, Bake My Day) in a permanent dual space. Notably, the mall is open to non-residents despite sitting inside a gated community, an experience choice worth watching as more UAE developers build neighbourhood-scale retail. Ajman completes UAE's first "headless" Agentic AI government transaction

AI & techUAEAI / tech23 Jul 2026 · recent

The Government of Ajman, via Digital Ajman, completed the UAE's first trade-licence renewal handled end-to-end by Agentic AI, a proactive, "headless" service model in which the system identifies a customer's need, maps the service journey, requests approvals and coordinates across departments (including Ajman Municipality and the Planning Department for lease-dependent renewals) with no human intervention. Customers are notified ahead of expiry and directed into the intelligent assistant inside the unified AjmanOne app to complete renewal. Corroborated independently by The National, Khaleej Times and Gulf News.

UAE Ministry of Finance call centre beats every H1 2026 target on generative AI

AI & techUAECX23 Jul 2026 · recent

The Ministry of Finance reported that generative-AI integration into its call centre pushed First Contact Resolution to 97.11%, cut Average Speed of Answer to 8 seconds, lifted Customer Happiness to 95.43% and Service Quality to 92.41%, while limiting Call Abandonment to 1.88%, all beating approved H1 2026 targets. A concrete, quantified government benchmark that GCC contact-centre operators can use to gauge realistic gains from generative-AI deployment.
