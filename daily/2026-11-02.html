Bishkek's new Ala-Too market opens on 1 November to take the Madina bazaar's traders

DestinationsKyrgyzstan · BishkekOpening1 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium→ 2027 Directory: Check first

The market built to replace Bishkek's Madina bazaar opened on 1 Nov 2026 under the name Ala-Too, at 2a/8 Shabdan Baatyr Avenue next to the old market. Gazeta.kg and NAQ Media both give 14 hectares, more than 1,500 trading points and parking for almost 1,000 cars, with heated, covered pavilions replacing containers for year-round trade. The complex passed to state control earlier in October; neither outlet names an operator, and Gazeta.kg reports traders' complaints about rents.

Why it matters: It settles a watch-list item with the market's official name, opening date and size, and is a candidate entry for Kyrgyzstan in the 2027 Directory.

Turkish mall sales per sqm rise 29.7% in September, matching 29.73% inflation, as entertainment leads

DestinationsTürkiyeMarket data28 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

The AYD-Akademetre mall productivity index rose 29.7% year on year in nominal terms in September 2026, against annual inflation of 29.73%, to 5,531 points; turnover per sqm was 21,085 TL nationally, 25,086 TL in Istanbul and 18,417 TL in Anatolia. Footfall rose 3.29% year on year but fell 2.08% on August. Entertainment and hobby (+50.2%) and technology (+43.5%) led the categories, with hypermarkets lowest at +12.1%. For Q3 the index rose 25.76% to 5,194 points. Mall Report and AVM Dergi carry matching figures; AYD's own index page did not yet list September on 2 Nov.

Why it matters: Turkish mall sales are only keeping pace with inflation while entertainment outgrows every other category, a data point for experience-led leasing.

Cenomi Centers signs a SAR 825m, 30-month design-and-build contract for the Madinah Downtown mall and offices

MembersSaudi Arabia · MadinahDeal / JV1 Nov 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Add to pipeline

Cenomi Centers told Tadawul on 1 Nov that on 29 Oct it signed a design-and-build contract worth SAR 825 million, excluding VAT, with Lynx Contracting Company for the mall and office components of Madinah Downtown, a project owned by Saudi Downtown Company, a PIF company. The 30-month contract covers design, engineering, construction, supply, installation, testing, commissioning and handover. Board members have interests in Lynx, so it is a related-party transaction that goes to the general assembly.

It follows the promise-to-lease and development services agreements Cenomi signed with Saudi Downtown for the mall in August. No GLA, mall name or opening date was given.

Why it matters: The Madinah Downtown mall, already on the 2027 Directory tracker, moves from a lease agreement to a contracted build with a 30-month programme.

Jabal Omar swings to a SAR 261.61m Q3 loss as land-sale gains drop out; nine-month profit falls 98.8%

MembersSaudi Arabia · MakkahFinancial1 Nov 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: High

Jabal Omar's Tadawul filing of 1 Nov shows nine-month 2026 revenue up 16% to SAR 1,824.34 million but net profit attributable to shareholders down 98.8% to SAR 13.51 million, from SAR 1,101.73 million. Q3 revenue rose 14.2% to SAR 370.03 million on hotels, commercial centres and the Rotana hotel opened at the start of the year, yet the quarter swung to a net loss of SAR 261.61 million from a SAR 197.85 million profit.

The company puts the fall mainly down to SAR 1,414 million less in one-off land-sale gains over nine months, higher finance costs on Phase 4 assets and higher depreciation, partly offset by lower impairments; commercial-centre revenue rose SAR 23 million over the nine months.

Why it matters: Jabal Omar's malls and hotels by the Grand Mosque are a member asset, and the filing shows the commercial centres growing even as headline profit collapses without land sales.

Saudi Arabia lets GCC and other visitors pre-clear land-border entry in Absher, mandatory after six months

CXSaudi ArabiaLaunch26 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: High

The Ministry of Interior has launched 'Absher Travel for Visitors' in the Absher app for GCC nationals and other visitors entering through all of the Kingdom's land border crossings. Visitors complete entry requirements before reaching the border, including traveller and vehicle details and vehicle insurance, to speed up entry; the service was built with a dozen government bodies and becomes mandatory six months after launch.

Why it matters: Faster, pre-cleared border crossings matter to GCC shoppers who drive into Saudi border-region malls, such as those in Al Khobar and Dammam, for weekends and holidays.

DoorDash agrees to buy Instacart for about $17bn

AI & techGlobalDeal / JV29 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

DoorDash agreed on 29 Oct to acquire grocery-delivery platform Instacart in a deal valued at about $17 billion, or $65.00 per Instacart share, a 26% premium to the 22 Oct close, the last trading day before reports of a possible deal. The companies expect it to close in late 2027 or the first half of 2028; DoorDash plans to keep the Instacart brand and app and offer one membership covering DashPass and Instacart+.

Why it matters: Combining a delivery network with grocery fulfilment technology and one membership is the model Gulf grocers and delivery apps measure their quick-commerce and loyalty plans against.

Good Morning America (ABC News) · 29 Oct 2026 ↗B
