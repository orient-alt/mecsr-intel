Aldar unveils Yas Point: AED 6bn ($1.63bn) waterfront destination with 700m retail promenade 10 Jul 2026

DestinationsUAE10 Jul 2026 · in window

MECSR member Aldar launched Yas Point, a 600,000 sqm waterfront development on the northern shore of Yas Island: circa 1,600 branded residences, a five-star resort hotel, a school, and retail, dining and leisure outlets organised around a 700-metre promenade activated by retail, dining and public spaces , plus two public parks and the island's first north-shore beach. Positioned near the upcoming Disneyland Abu Dhabi and Sphere Abu Dhabi, it extends Yas Island's retail-and-leisure cluster. Retail GLA has not yet been disclosed, a candidate for the directory pipeline once leasing details emerge.

MEMBER NEWS Knowledge Economic City signs $400m fund term sheet with Albilad Capital: Multaqa Mall connectivity Deal / JV 13 Jul 2026

MembersSaudi ArabiaDeal / JV13 Jul 2026 · in windowMECSR member

Priority-1 member Knowledge Economic City Co disclosed to Tadawul a non-binding term sheet with Albilad Capital to establish a closed-ended private real estate fund financing phase 1 of its Multaqa Hospitality project in Madinah, development value ~SAR 1.5bn ($400m), comprising a 288-key JW Marriott, a 327-key Marriott and ~295 branded residences on King Abdulaziz Road. Directly relevant to the retail directory: the cluster will be connected to Multaqa Mall , the Hilton Hotel Tower and Hilton Branded Residences via a pedestrian skybridge, reinforcing the mall's anchor position in the Multaqa Almadinah masterplan. Albilad Capital would act as fund manager, subject to regulatory approvals; a six-month exclusivity period applies.

MEMBER NEWS Dalma Mall receives Platinum Impact Seal: UAE's highest federal CSR honour Award 8 Jul 2026

MembersUAEAward8 Jul 2026 · recentMECSR member
