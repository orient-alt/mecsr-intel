Sahl digital bill-payment platform extended to Egyptians living abroad

CXEgyptCX8 Aug 2026 · in window

Egypt has expanded its Sahl application so citizens overseas, including the large Egyptian communities across the GCC, can pay utility and government-related bills remotely, with no need to be physically present in Egypt or rely on family intermediaries. For MECSC members this is a clean example of a government extending a domestic self-service channel to a diaspora customer base, removing one of the most common cross-border service pain points in the region.

CKYC 2.0: NRIs in the UAE to escape repeat KYC checks with Indian banks from August

CXUAECXAug 2026 · recent

India's upgraded Central KYC registry (CKYC 2.0), rolling out from August 2026, will let non-resident Indians in the UAE reuse verified KYC records across Indian banks and insurers instead of repeating document checks with every institution. With NRIs a major banking segment in the UAE, this is a meaningful customer-effort reduction story for cross-border financial services, the kind of interoperability regional banks are increasingly measured against.
