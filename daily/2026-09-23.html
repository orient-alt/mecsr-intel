CPPE flags growing foreign-national expansion into Nigeria's basic retail trade

DestinationsNigeriaMarket data20 Sep 2026 · in window

The Centre for the Promotion of Private Enterprise (CPPE), a Lagos-based economic think tank, publicly raised concern over the expanding presence of Chinese and other foreign nationals in Nigeria's basic retail trade, textiles, ICT accessories, auto parts, tyres, electricals and plumbing, arguing that unregulated foreign participation is squeezing indigenous SMEs out of segments the country's business-permit framework intends to reserve for Nigerians. CPPE called on the Federal Government to review business-permit issuance and expatriate-quota allocations in these trade categories. The story names no specific mall, shopping centre or MECSR member company; it is a market/policy-level finding relevant to Nigeria's retail-trade operating environment, including for mall-based Nigerian retailers.

Al-Futtaim Group partners with Juspay for unified payment orchestration across 50 brands

MembersUAEDeal / JV22 Sep 2026 · in windowMECSR member

Al-Futtaim Group (member), through its Al-Futtaim Finance arm, has partnered with Indian payments-technology company Juspay to serve as its payment-orchestration provider across the group's retail, automotive and other consumer-facing brands. The rollout begins in September 2026, with the first brand going live this month, and will eventually cover 50 brands across 12 markets. The platform centralises payment routing, fraud management, 3-D Secure workflows, local payment methods and analytics while retaining country- and brand-level configuration. Al-Futtaim Finance CEO Omar Haddad described the goal as "a simpler, more seamless and secure way for customers to transact." Both companies frame the deployment as infrastructure for future AI-driven agentic commerce, though the current rollout is a traditional payment-orchestration upgrade rather than autonomous purchasing.

TTEC opens new Cairo operation for Volkswagen Group UK

CXEgyptExpansion22 Sep 2026 · in window

TTEC launched a new customer-experience operation in Cairo dedicated to supporting Volkswagen Group UK's automotive customers, part of a seven-year strategic collaboration announced earlier in 2026. The hub delivers multilingual digital and voice customer care, TTEC's Egypt operation already supports 11 languages including Arabic, English, French, German, Italian and Spanish, leveraging what the companies describe as "AI-enabled CX capabilities" alongside automotive-sector expertise. VWG UK's Head of Customer Management, Chris Stevens, said the Cairo launch "gives us access to skilled talent and capabilities that will help us deliver consistent, high-quality support," while TTEC Engage CEO John Abou pointed to the combination of "a highly skilled workforce, AI-enabled technology and decades of automotive expertise." This is the third Cairo-based contact-centre expansion logged in successive dossier cycles, after ibex's 1,000-seat City Stars centre and InteLogix's New Cairo facility, and the first specifically anchored to a named automotive OEM relationship rather than a generic multi-sector BPO build-out. TTEC previously flagged plans to add 3,500 Egypt roles by 2029; this launch is an early, named data point against that target.

UAE Central Bank confirms unified "golden source" document platform

CXUAECX22 Sep 2026 · in window

The UAE Central Bank, working with the UAE Banks Federation and other stakeholders, confirmed plans for a centralised digital system that automatically updates customer personal information and documents across multiple banks, targeted to launch before year-end 2026. The platform runs on a "golden source" principle, retrieving data electronically from authoritative government sources rather than requiring customers to resubmit documents such as Emirates ID or passport each time they expire or renew. UBF Director General Jamal Saleh framed the goal as being "to collect and link customer information electronically," giving customers consolidated access to linked services in a single place instead of repeating the same paperwork at every bank. This is a direct friction-reduction play for retail-banking CX rather than a security or compliance story, and complements the Central Bank's earlier e-KYC platform work, worth tracking as a concrete, dated example of "reduce repeated effort" service design that MECSC members in banking and government-adjacent sectors may want to benchmark against their own onboarding and renewal journeys.

Al-Futtaim taps Juspay for payment orchestration across 50 brands, 12 markets

AI & techGlobalAI / tech23 Sep 2026 · in window

Dubai-based Al-Futtaim confirmed a partnership with India-headquartered Juspay to deploy a unified payment-orchestration platform across 50 of its 200-plus brands (spanning automotive, financial services, real estate, retail and healthcare) in 12 markets, with initial rollout underway this month. The orchestration layer centralises payment routing, fraud prevention, adaptive authentication, regional payment methods, tokenisation, analytics and reconciliation, and integrates Juspay's Breeze checkout stack, while preserving brand-level flexibility through tiered, country- and brand-specific configuration rather than forcing a single uniform checkout experience. Both companies pointed to agentic commerce as a longer-term capability the infrastructure is built to support, though the current phase is conventional orchestration. The story was independently corroborated by GCC Business News, CFOtech Asia and TechEdt, giving it solid cross-referenceability for a vendor-partnership announcement. It also lands the same week Al-Futtaim's Executive Director of Technology & AI Strategy, Harshdeep Jolly, was scheduled to keynote on agentic AI at Seamless Middle East 2026 (Dubai, 22–24 Sep), see Watch List for follow-up on what else emerges from that event, including Al-Futtaim's existing Blue AI lifestyle-advisor, Tally shelf-scanning robot and TAFFI "Amira" stylist deployments.
