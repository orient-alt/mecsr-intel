Esas Gayrimenkul widens non-rent income at its Turkish malls, from media and pop-ups to parking, EV charging and solar

DestinationsTürkiye · Various (Çanakkale named)Market data19 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Nezaket Akçakaya, senior director of property management at Esas Gayrimenkul, told Mall Report (19 Nov) that its shopping centres earn non-rent income from common-area leasing, advertising and digital screens, events and sponsorship, kiosks, pop-ups and seasonal sales points, parking, EV charging and solar installations, with parking and EV charging run on space-leasing and revenue-share models. Why it matters: Income from media, parking, EV charging and solar is a live topic for GCC mall owners, and the interview sets out how one Turkish manager runs these lines.

Dubai ranks first among cities in the UN's Local Online Service Index 2026, with a score of 100%

CXUAE · DubaiAward19 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Dubai ranked first globally in the Local Online Service Index (LOSI) 2026, part of the 2026 UN E-Government Survey by the UN Department of Economic and Social Affairs, with a perfect score of 100%, Dubai Media Office reported on 19 Nov. The city has risen from 16th in 2018 and 2020 to fifth in 2022, joint fourth in 2024 and first in 2026; the index rates city-level online services on 101 indicators across six criteria. The survey's theme is 'From Digital Government to Agentic Government', and Hamad Obaid Al Mansoori, Director General of Digital Dubai, is quoted; the release does not say how many cities were assessed.

Why it matters: The UN's benchmark for city-level online services sets the standard that Dubai residents now hold every service provider to, malls, retailers and banks included, and its theme points to AI agents as the next phase of service.

2

Salesforce forecasts that AI and agents will influence $75.8bn, or 21%, of global Cyber Week online sales (vendor forecast)

AI & techGlobalMarket data19 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Salesforce's own forecast, drawn from its Shopping Index of more than 1.5 billion shoppers, expects global online sales of $357bn in Cyber Week (24 to 30 Nov 2026), up 6% year on year, with $75.8bn, or 21%, influenced by AI and agents, against $67bn in 2025. It expects AI-driven traffic to rise 104% year on year and 20% of holiday shopping traffic to be nonhuman, and says shoppers referred by AI are twice as likely to convert. The Middle East and Africa is one of the index's 18 markets, but the forecast gives no regional figures.

Why it matters: Gulf retailers run their own late-November sales and the Dubai Shopping Festival opens on 4 Dec, so the share of traffic arriving through AI assistants and agents bears on how they measure and serve online demand.

Visa opens Agentic Ready to merchants, acquirers and payment providers, starting with more than 140 participants

AI & techGlobalLaunch17 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Visa has extended Agentic Ready, part of Visa Intelligent Commerce, from card issuers to the acceptance side, so that merchants, payment service providers, acquirers and commerce platforms can prepare for purchases started by AI agents (release dated 17 Nov). The programme launches there with more than 140 participants, among them Adyen, Checkout.com, Stripe, PayPal, Worldline, Fiserv and Ant International, alongside more than 175 issuers across 45 markets, and more are expected to join throughout 2027. Why it matters: It brings a card network's preparation for agent-led payments to the merchant side, the open question on the agent-led shopping watch item, and UAE banks and fintechs are already in the issuer phase.
