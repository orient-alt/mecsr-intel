Saudi Retail Forum moves from 19 Nov 2026 to March 2027

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · RiyadhEvent19 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Article idea

The Saudi Retail Forum, due in Riyadh on 19 Nov 2026, has been postponed to March 2027, The National reported on 19 Oct in its round-up of Riyadh events moved after Houthi missile and drone attacks on the kingdom.

The forum's website, run by Images RetailME, now carries a banner saying the event moves to March 2027. When checked on 20 Oct it gave no new day, venue or reason.

Why it matters: A leading Riyadh retail-industry forum leaving the fourth-quarter calendar changes the speaker, sponsor and delegate picture for MECSR's own Saudi events, including RCM Jeddah on 7 and 8 Dec.

FII10 is postponed to 2027 and Intersec Saudi Arabia moves to October 2027

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · RiyadhEvent19 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

The FII Institute said on 19 Oct 2026 that the tenth Future Investment Initiative, due in Riyadh from 26 to 29 Oct, will now be held in 2027, with new dates to be announced; Gulf Business reports that existing registrations and partnerships remain valid.

The National links the move to Houthi missile and drone attacks on the kingdom, including strikes on King Khalid International Airport. It also reports that Intersec Saudi Arabia, due from 16 to 18 Nov, will now be held in October 2027, according to its organiser Messe Frankfurt Saudi Arabia, and that a planned Saudi-EU summit has been postponed.

Why it matters: Riyadh loses its largest business events of the quarter, which matters for hotel, mall and F&B traffic around the capital's venues and for planning around RCM Jeddah.

Emirates NBD to run OpenAI's GPT models with in-country UAE inference, billed as a first for a MENA bank

AI & techUAE · DubaiDeal / JV19 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Emirates NBD and OpenAI announced an enterprise collaboration on 19 Oct 2026 under which the bank will use OpenAI's GPT models, tools and in-country inference infrastructure in the UAE.

The bank plans to use them for customer engagement across its digital channels, risk and compliance analytics, knowledge-heavy workflows and staff productivity, connected to its internal systems, APIs and data. Gulf News reports it as the first such arrangement for a bank in the MENA region; no financial terms were published.

Why it matters: A major UAE bank is putting generative AI into customer-facing channels while keeping data in the country, a reference point for members weighing AI in customer service against data-residency rules.

American Express publishes an Intent Intelligence framework for purchases made by AI shopping agents

AI & techGlobalLaunch13 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: High

American Express published its Intent Intelligence framework on 13 Oct 2026. It sets out how a shopper's instructions, such as the merchant and a maximum budget, are captured and approved before an AI agent buys, then passed to the card issuer as an Intent ID and a Proof of Intent Token that can be exchanged for a limited payment credential used at checkout instead of the card number.

The records can then support fraud checks, authorisation decisions and disputes. The framework is offered through the Amex Agentic Commerce Experiences (ACE) Developer Kit.

Why it matters: It gives retailers and their service teams an early model for proving what a customer approved when an agent buys on their behalf, which will matter for refunds and disputes as agent-led shopping reaches the Gulf.
