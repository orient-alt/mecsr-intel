Fashion House Group to develop and run an international outlet at Grand Uptown Mall, New October City, from October 2028

DestinationsEgypt · New October CityNew property1 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium→ 2027 Directory: Watch

Uptown 6 October for Development and Investment has signed a memorandum under which UK-based Fashion House Group will develop and operate an international outlet at Grand Uptown Mall in New October City, with Emtelak Real Estate Investment Fund as strategic partner. The scheme, billed as North Africa's first integrated outlet destination, adds a boutique hotel, cinema, restaurants and an e-commerce platform with click and collect; phase 1 is scheduled for October 2028, subject to designs and approvals. Daily News Egypt reports an investment of over EGP 6bn and about 90,000 sqm of leasable space, figures no second source confirms yet.

Why it matters: Egypt's first planned outlet destination with an international outlet operator, and a new pipeline lead for the 2027 Directory.

Emaar The Economic City's Q3 loss widens to SAR 241m as revenue falls 59.5%

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · King Abdullah Economic CityFinancial1 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

Emaar The Economic City, developer of King Abdullah Economic City, posted a Q3 2026 net loss of SAR 241 million against SAR 135 million a year earlier, as revenue fell 59.5% to SAR 145 million, according to Argaam's report of its filing. The nine-month loss widened 26.5% to SAR 444 million on revenue down 37.0% to SAR 429 million, mainly on lower project revenue, higher legal provisions and a one-off financing gain in 2025, partly offset by lower financing costs and a gain on selling investment properties.

Why it matters: The sharp fall in project revenue at King Abdullah Economic City's developer belongs in the Q3 results picture ahead of RCM Jeddah (7 to 8 Dec).

Waitrose signs as the first retail tenant of DMCC's Uptown Place, due in Q1 2028

DestinationsUAE · DubaiTenant news3 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Watch

DMCC has signed Spinneys to open a Waitrose store of over 5,000 sq ft, a smaller format with a food-to-go deli, at Uptown Place in Uptown Dubai. It is the first confirmed tenant of the scheme's 82,000 sq ft of retail space across 18 units, alongside more than 560,000 sq ft of Grade A offices in two towers and 1,600 parking spaces; Uptown Place is expected to open in Q1 2028.

Why it matters: A grocery-anchored retail podium in a Dubai business district, a small pipeline entry and an example of convenience retail built into office schemes.

Seef Properties' nine-month profit falls 30% to BD 2.68m as regional events weigh on leisure and spending

MembersBahrain · ManamaFinancial4 Nov 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: High

Seef Properties reported net profit attributable to shareholders of BD 2.68 million for the nine months to 30 Sep 2026, down 30.0% from BD 3.82 million, on total revenue of BD 9.65 million, down 12.7%; third-quarter profit fell 30.7% to BD 0.77 million on revenue down 12.4% to BD 3.08 million. The company blames regional developments that hit hospitality and entertainment and weighed on consumer spending.

It says the Seef Mall - Seef District redevelopment continues while the mall trades and its Dammam mixed-use project with Majd Investment is progressing.

Why it matters: A member mall owner puts filed figures on how the regional security situation is hitting mall leisure and spending in Bahrain.

Customer experience & service

1

UAE's latest government service star ratings cover 179 channels; Fujairah's traffic centre earns the top 7 stars

CXUAECX4 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid approved and announced on 4 Nov the results of the latest cycle of the Global Star Rating System, which assessed 179 service channels across 27 federal entities on a two-to-seven-star scale using independent audits, mystery shoppers and customer feedback. The Ministry of Interior's Traffic and Licensing Services Centre in Fujairah received 7 stars. The National also reports gains of 13% for call centres, 5% for service centres and 4% for digital channels.

Why it matters: It is the UAE's benchmark for public-service quality, built on the mystery shopping, channel audits and customer feedback that MECSC applies in retail.

AI & retail technology

1

Amazon opens its Alexa for Shopping agent to outside merchants through a Shop Direct workspace

AI & techGlobalLaunch4 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Amazon has added a Shop Direct Merchant Workspace that lets eligible merchants register, link their online store and submit product feeds so their products can be found through Alexa for Shopping and Amazon's website and app; shoppers tap 'Shop Direct' and finish the purchase on the merchant's own site. Andy Jassy told analysts that Alexa for Shopping 'is going to be the primary shopping agent customers use to shop Amazon'. Regions and start date were not given.

Why it matters: Amazon, which blocked outside AI shopping agents in October, is now drawing outside retailers into its own agent, a model Gulf retailers on Amazon.ae, Amazon.sa and Amazon.eg should watch.
