Al-Andalus Mall's Jadat Al-Andalus expansion in Jeddah is fully leased, and Alandalus Property's Q3 retail revenue rose 18%

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · JeddahExpansion8 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Alandalus Property's chief executive Faisal Alnasser told Argaam that Al-Andalus Mall in Jeddah performed strongly after leasing of its new expansion, Jadat Al-Andalus, was completed. He said retail revenue rose 18% year on year in Q3 2026 and average occupancy across the company's managed retail portfolio was about 99.5%, while the DoubleTree by Hilton at the mall raised revenue 62% after development works. No GLA or opening date for the expansion was given.

Why it matters: A fully let expansion and near-full occupancy at an established Jeddah mall, in the city that hosts RCM Jeddah on 7 to 8 Dec.

JYSK opens its 18th Türkiye store at Mall of İstanbul and plans 12 more stores in 2027

DestinationsTürkiye · IstanbulTenant news6 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Home furnishings retailer JYSK has opened a store at Mall of İstanbul, its 8th in Istanbul and 18th in Türkiye (AVM Dergi, 6 Nov; Mall Report, 7 Nov). Country director Fatih Tezcan says JYSK will open 12 new stores in 2027 to reach cities and regions where it has no stores yet. JYSK entered Türkiye through direct investment in May 2023 and runs more than 3,700 stores in 49 countries. Neither report gives the store's size or exact opening day.

Why it matters: A tenant addition at a listed Istanbul mall, and a 12-store 2027 pipeline that Turkish mall leasing teams will compete for.

Elya Center AVM in Ankara's Çankaya district starts leasing, with Asüer Danışmanlık as leasing consultant

CBRE: mall occupancy at 97% in Dubai and 96% in Abu Dhabi in Q3, with about 240,000 sqm of retail GLA due in 2027

DestinationsUAE · DubaiMarket data3 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

CBRE's UAE Real Estate Market Review for Q3 2026 (3 Nov) puts retail occupancy at 97% in Dubai and 96% in Abu Dhabi, with average retail rents up 5% year on year in Dubai and 1% in Abu Dhabi. It expects no major Dubai completions in 2026, noting that The Grand at Nad Al Sheba Gardens began a phased opening in August fully leased, and says nearly 240,000 sqm of retail GLA is scheduled for handover in 2027, including 'Skylight Mall (formerly Al Khail Avenue)' at approximately 111,000 sqm GLA. Other Dubai schemes it names for Q4 2026 and 2027 are First Avenue Mall Jumeirah, Le Meryeme Malls (The Villa and Liwan), Sobha Mall at Sobha Hartland and CMPLX Al Quoz.

Why it matters: Near-full occupancy, rising Dubai rents and a modest 2027 pipeline set the benchmark for leasing talks and the Directory pipeline, and CBRE identifies Skylight Mall as the former Al Khail Avenue.

Lulu Retail's Q3 revenue rises 2% to $1.9bn as online sales jump 53%, and most planned stores will be small formats

MembersUAE · Abu DhabiFinancial6 Nov 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: Medium

Khaleej Times reports that Lulu Retail's revenue rose 2% year on year to $1.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, with EBITDA of $175 million and net income of $29 million, as higher input costs weighed on gross margins. Online sales rose 53% to 9.7% of retail sales, and the loyalty programme reached 10 million members across the GCC. Lulu opened five stores in Q3 (two in Saudi Arabia and one each in the UAE, Oman and Kuwait), adding about 18,800 square metres, for 22 in the first nine months; of the stores planned for 2027 to 2031, about a third are in the UAE and a third in Saudi Arabia, and more than three-quarters are expected to be smaller Express or Mini Market formats. Lulu's own release was not available to check the figures.

Why it matters: A member that anchors many GCC malls is moving most future openings to small Express and Mini Market formats, which changes the anchor space it will take in new schemes.

Saudi healthcare bodies win 36 awards at the International Customer Experience Awards 2026 in London

CXSaudi ArabiaAward8 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Saudi healthcare entities won 36 gold, silver and bronze awards at the International Customer Experience Awards 2026 in London, 29 of them for Health Holding Company and its health clusters, according to an SPA report of 7 Nov carried by Asharq Al-Awsat and Arab News on 8 Nov. Riyadh First Health Cluster led with 11 awards, the Madinah and Eastern clusters took six each, Al-Ahsa five and the Ministry of Health's Najran branch four. The Security Forces Hospital in Dammam, run by the Ministry of Interior's medical services, won gold for Best Contact Center, and Arab News adds gold CX Leader of the Year awards for Madinah Health Cluster chief executive Abdulrahman Al-Harbi and Dr Ibraheem Bani Hameem of the Najran branch.

Why it matters: Saudi public healthcare is now a leading entrant at international CX awards, a benchmark for MECSC's customer-service awards, training and contact-centre work in the Kingdom.

Dubai Access Club members get a Majid Al Futtaim gift card for retail spending, the only retail partner named so far

CXUAE · DubaiLaunch5 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Dubai's invitation-only Dubai Access Club, developed by GDRFA Dubai and the Department of Economy and Tourism, gives frequent visitors a five-year multiple-entry tourist visa, with invitations issued through selected loyalty programmes and multinational companies linked to the launch partners. Members receive GDRFA's ALSAADA Tourist Card and a Majid Al Futtaim gift card with credit for participating retail stores and destinations, plus benefits for Visa Infinite cardholders and some Emirates Skywards members, and more partners are due to join. The National lists Emirates, Jumeirah, Accor, Hilton, IHG, Hyatt and Taj as hospitality partners and U by Emaar among the accepted loyalty programmes, and names no individual malls.

Why it matters: Majid Al Futtaim gets first access to the club's high-value repeat visitors, and with more partners due to join, other mall operators and retailers have an opening.
