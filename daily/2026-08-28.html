First Avenue Mall, Jumeirah heads for a September opening built around dining, not fashion

DestinationsUAE17 Aug 2026 · recent

First Avenue Mall in Jumeirah is on track for a September 2026 opening with a tenant mix led by restaurants, Jon & Vinny's, Nala (from the team behind Tashas) and Lost in Tokyo among the confirmed names, rather than the fashion-anchor format typical of earlier Dubai malls. Le Meryeme's two community malls bet on everyday services over fashion retail

DestinationsUAE17 Aug 2026 · recent

Two Le Meryeme community malls are in Dubai's near-term pipeline: The Villa location (Q3/September 2026) with a gym, rooftop pool and a retail mix including barbers, a coffee/cake concept and a Carnival soft-play area for children; and Liwan (Q4 2026), built around a hypermarket, medical centre, sports areas, kids' spaces and an outdoor events plaza. stc Bahrain promotes Khalid Alia to Chief Digital and Customer Experience Officer

CXBahrainLeadership20 Aug 2026 · recent

stc Bahrain named Khalid Alia, previously Senior Director of Consumer Marketing and Customer Experience, where he drove consumer strategy and several product/service launches, to the newly elevated role of Chief Digital and Customer Experience Officer. He brings international telecom sector experience across commercial and marketing leadership roles and will now oversee stc Bahrain's digital transformation agenda alongside its customer-focused strategy, with an explicit brief to accelerate digital adoption and build solutions around changing customer demands. A useful regional data point: this is the second Gulf telecom CX-leadership elevation MECSC has tracked in recent weeks (following similar structural moves at other operators), suggesting operators are formally merging the "digital" and "customer experience" mandates into one seat rather than running them as separate functions.

Dubai Airports and GDRFA brace for the post-summer travel surge with a "border hospitality" push

CXUAECX17 Aug 2026 · recent

As Dubai International handles the wave of returning holidaymakers, GDRFA Dubai has deployed additional frontline officers and technical support units, opened dedicated arrival channels and express corridors, and trained border personnel in what it calls "border hospitality", balancing security with a service-first tone. The agency is coordinating closely with Dubai Airports, Dubai Police, customs and airlines to keep queues moving through the surge. The push builds on infrastructure already in place: Dubai's biometric Smart Gates processed 9.4 million travellers in H1 2026 (9,024,736 through Smart Gates, 439,321 through the "Red Carpet" corridor), with Red Carpet clearance time cut to 3.4 seconds from 12.5 seconds, a 60% reduction achieved with zero documents and zero physical touchpoints. A July addition, the Smart Gates Eligibility Pre-Check tool (via the Pocket Flights portal or in-terminal QR codes), lets travellers confirm Smart Gates eligibility on their phone before reaching passport control, aimed at DXB's peak days of 265,000+ daily passengers. For MECSC members in aviation, hospitality and government service delivery, it's a clear example of pairing automation (biometrics, self-check tools) with a deliberate human-service layer for the moments automation can't smooth over.

CXUAECX29 Jul 2026 · recent

Emirates now accepts Crypto.com Pay for AED flight bookings on emirates.com and the Emirates App, a regulator-compliant option positioned against Dubai's Cashless Strategy target of 90% digital transactions in 2026. Five9, Cisco and Meta show three different stages of agentic CX maturity

AI & techGlobalMarket data14 Aug 2026 · recent

Global item, included for relevance to GCC contact-centre and retail buyers. Three simultaneous data points from CX Today's roundup sketch where agentic AI in customer service actually stands. Five9 signed a five-year, US$100m CCaaS contract with a Fortune 100 financial services firm, ramping to US$25m in annual recurring revenue, transacted through the Google Cloud Marketplace, a channel-fit test as much as a product win. Cisco disclosed that its agentic AI independently resolved 145,000 support cases with zero human intervention in FY2026, with CEO Chuck Robbins tying this to faster quote turnaround, case resolution and higher renewal rates rather than headcount reduction. Meta's Mark Zuckerberg, meanwhile, previewed a consumer-side shift: personal "superintelligent" AI agents that manage information and screen brand interactions on a customer's behalf. The practical read for MECSC members: the vendor conversation has moved from "does agentic AI work" (Cisco's case-resolution number) to "will it scale commercially" (Five9's marketplace-routed deal) to "what happens when customers put their own AI agent in front of yours" (Meta's framing), three different maturity questions that GCC service and retail leaders should expect to be asked about in the same vendor pitch within the next year.

Liferay unveils AI Hub ahead of LEAP 2026, betting Saudi enterprises are past the "whether" question

AI & techSaudi ArabiaAI / tech24 Aug 2026 · recent

Liferay is heading into LEAP 2026 (Riyadh, 31 Aug–3 Sep, Booth H4.E70) with two flagship offerings: AI Hub, a standalone low-code SaaS product for building, deploying and managing AI agents while keeping them grounded in an organisation's own data and existing access controls; and a Data Platform giving B2B teams unified account intelligence from CRM, marketing automation and behavioural data. The framing from Moussalam Dalati, Liferay's MEA/France General Manager, is the sharper point for MECSC members: "the priority now is making AI work, securely, governed and grounded in the right data to produce business value", i.e. Saudi enterprise buyers have largely moved past pilots and are being sold on governance and data-grounding as the differentiator, not raw AI capability. Worth watching whether this framing repeats across other LEAP 2026 exhibitors (MIT KSA is also confirmed to showcase AI/digital innovation) once the event opens next week.
