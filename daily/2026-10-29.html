Meta launches its Muse shopping and task agent in Singapore, with Shopee, Grab and Lazada among the partners

AI & techGlobalLaunch28 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Meta made Muse, its personal AI agent, available in Singapore on 28 Oct on iOS, Android and muse.ai, with Shopee, Grab, Singapore Airlines, Agoda and ShopBack named at launch and Klook, Trip.com and Lazada as further partners. Muse compares products and prices, builds a Grab basket and places the order only after the user approves, and books Klook experiences in the chat; passwords and payment methods are kept out of the agent's view in a dedicated cloud environment.

Muse is free for most everyday uses, with paid plans for more, and connector features roll out over time. No Middle East launch was mentioned.

Why it matters: Marketplaces and delivery apps chose to plug into a consumer agent that buys only with the shopper's approval, the decision GCC retailers and mall apps face if Muse reaches the region.
