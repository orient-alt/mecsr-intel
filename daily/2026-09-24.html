Azerbaijan's retail trade turnover rises 3.9% in real terms, Jan–Aug 2026

DestinationsAzerbaijanMarket data11 Sep 2026 · recent

Azerbaijan's State Statistical Committee reported that retail trade turnover reached AZN44.3bn (US$26.1bn) in the first eight months of 2026, up 3.9% in real terms year-on-year. Food products accounted for AZN24.1bn (+1.4%) and non-food products AZN20.2bn (+6.9%). Computers and telecommunications equipment was the fastest-growing category (+33.4%), followed by pharmaceutical products (+21.6%). Average monthly per-capita retail spending was AZN538.6 (US$316.8). The story names no specific shopping destination or MECSR member company; it is a market-level finding relevant to Azerbaijan's retail operating environment.

Aldar–Mubadala joint venture acquires Masdar City Square for AED918 million

MembersUAEDeal / JV16 Sep 2026 · recentMECSR member

Aldar (member) and Mubadala's Masdar City joint venture acquired Masdar City Square, a seven-building commercial complex adding more than 47,000 sqm of net leasable office space, for AED918 million. The complex is 99% occupied, with tenants including TAQA, the Department of Energy, Emirates College and Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, and reached practical completion in Q1 2026. The acquisition expands the Aldar–Mubadala joint venture's total Masdar City real-estate portfolio to AED4.7 billion and over 158,000 sqm of office space. The buildings are designed to LEED Platinum and WELL Gold standards with solar photovoltaic systems and passive cooling. This is an office/commercial (not retail-anchored) acquisition; included here as a member-company portfolio deal rather than a shopping-destination finding.

Oman's government e-services satisfaction climbs to 90% in UN maturity index

CXOmanCX24 Jul 2026 · recent

Oman's overall score on the UN ESCWA Government Electronic and Mobile Services (GEMS) Maturity Index rose to 75% for 2025, up from 72% in 2024. Citizen satisfaction on the government's electronic portal climbed to 90% (from 89%), alongside 96% service uptime across digital channels, a 98% support-request response rate, and a 93% ease-of-use score. The assessment covered 27 institutions and 93 services spanning health, education, justice and municipal affairs, and also recorded gains in cross-entity integration (91%) and open-data availability (up from 80% to 89%). The underlying data covers 2025 performance, but its July 2026 publication is Oman's first dated government-CX data point logged in several dossier cycles, giving MECSC members a citizen-facing digital-service benchmark to set alongside the UAE and Saudi government-excellence programmes more frequently covered in this dossier.

Emirates NBD tops 2026 UAE bank rankings as FAB posts strongest gains

CXUAECX9 Sep 2026 · recent

YouGov's 2026 UAE Bank Rankings, which track which banks UAE adults would consider for a new account or product, put Emirates NBD in the lead with 29.2% consideration, ahead of ADCB (24.2%) and FAB (22%). Emirates NBD also recorded the UAE's highest net Recommendation Score at 75.3 among current and former customers, a direct measure of service-driven advocacy rather than brand awareness alone. FAB posted the strongest year-on-year improvement of the three, gaining 1.1 percentage points, while among Gen Z respondents newer digital-first entrants such as Mashreq Neo gained ground even as the same top-three ranking held overall. This is a customer-perception benchmark rather than an operational CX story, but the named recommendation-score and generational-preference data give MECSC members in retail banking and financial services a fresh, dated reference point for competitive positioning against the UAE's three largest banking brands.

Air India Express's "Tia" wins Best Use of AI in Customer Experience

AI & techGlobalAward23 Sep 2026 · in window

Air India Express won "Best Use of AI in Customer Experience" at the UBS Forums 25th CX Strategy Summit & Awards 2026 for Tia, its AI-powered guest-support platform. Tia resolves more than 75% of an estimated 15,000 daily customer queries through self-service, offering upwards of 100 self-service options, bookings, cancellations, refunds, baggage queries, across web, app, WhatsApp and contact-centre channels, with both chat and voice automation and full interaction-history handover when a query escalates to a human agent. First deployed in 2021 as India's first airline chatbot, the platform has since added multilingual and increasingly personalised support. Not a GCC deployment, but a directly comparable one: Emirates, Qatar Airways, Etihad and other regional carriers investing in AI-driven guest support now have a named, metrics-backed external benchmark, 75%+ self-service resolution at 15,000-daily-query scale, to measure their own virtual-assistant programmes against. Independently corroborated by TravelBiz Monitor, TTN Middle East and UNI India, giving it solid cross-referenceability for an awards story.

mazeed unveils "Matha," an AI financial agent, at Seamless Middle East 2026

AI & techMENAAI / tech23 Sep 2026 · in window

UAE accounting and e-invoicing platform mazeed launched Matha, a conversational AI financial agent, at Seamless Middle East 2026 in Dubai. Available as an iOS/Android app to all mazeed subscription tiers, Matha lets business owners ask natural-language questions about cash position and financial performance, and also works proactively, independently flagging unusual trends, payment delays and approaching tax deadlines rather than waiting to be asked. Beyond conversation, users can process invoices via WhatsApp, record payments, and scan documents for automated data extraction. The launch coincided with mazeed showcasing its wider e-invoicing compliance stack ahead of the UAE's mandatory shift to digital invoicing formats. The launch lands inside Seamless Middle East 2026's three-day run (22–24 Sep, Dubai World Trade Centre), the same event where Al-Futtaim's technology chief keynoted on agentic AI (logged in the 23 Sep dossier), reinforcing proactive, agentic finance AI as a recurring theme of this year's edition. Worth watching for further exhibitor announcements as the event closes.
