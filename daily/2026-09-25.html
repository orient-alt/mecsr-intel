Kazakhstan's wholesale trade turnover rises 6.7% in Jan–Aug 2026

DestinationsKazakhstanMarket data14 Sep 2026 · recent

GASTAT: Saudi wholesale & retail trade operating-revenues index up 3.2% in Q2 2026, retail e-sales +13.4%

DestinationsSaudi ArabiaMarket data30 Aug 2026 · recent

Saudi Arabia's General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT) reported its General Index of Operating Revenues for the wholesale and retail trade sector rose 3.2% year-on-year in Q2 2026 (down 0.6% quarter-on-quarter versus Q1). The e-commerce sales index grew 9.8% annually overall, with retail trade e-sales up 13.4% and wholesale trade e-sales up 6.5%; the employee compensation index rose 9.0% annually, driven by a 10.8% increase in retail trade wages. The release names no specific shopping destination or MECSR member company; it is a national market-level indicator relevant to Saudi Arabia's retail operating environment.

Knowledge Economic City signs SAR2.8bn ($746m) Madinah mixed-use JV with Dar Al Majdiah and Capital Hill Financial

MembersSaudi ArabiaNew property15 Sep 2026 · recentMECSR member

Knowledge Economic City Company (KEC, member) signed a memorandum of understanding with Dar Al Majdiah Real Estate Co. and Capital Hill Financial Company to develop a mixed-use real-estate project on roughly 97,000 sqm within KEC's Madinah masterplan. The development will comprise approximately 2,700 residential apartments, primarily targeting non-Saudi Muslim buyers, plus a commercial component with a net leasable area of around 8,000 sqm and more than 3,500 parking spaces. The estimated total project cost is approximately SAR2.8bn ($746.25m); KEC anticipates revenue exceeding SAR4bn. The venture will be structured through a closed-ended real-estate investment fund in which KEC holds roughly 80% (contributing land in kind) and Al Majdiah roughly 20% (contributing cash and serving as development manager), with Capital Hill establishing and managing the fund. This is primarily a residential-led mixed-use project; the confirmed 8,000 sqm retail/commercial component is modest relative to a standalone mall and is flagged here as a member-company project update rather than a new shopping-destination finding.

DCT Abu Dhabi rolls out Visitor Experience Guidelines and an AI-powered training academy

CXUAECX19 Sep 2026 · recent

The Department of Culture and Tourism – Abu Dhabi (DCT Abu Dhabi) introduced destination-wide Visitor Experience Guidelines spanning 11 tourism touchpoints, from airports and hotels to museums and attractions, setting consistent service-quality expectations across the emirate's tourism ecosystem. Alongside the guidelines, DCT Abu Dhabi launched the Visitor Experience (VX) Academy, an AI-powered e-learning initiative delivering role-specific professional-development training to frontline tourism workers. The academy has already enrolled 38,643 learners from 260 participating organisations, including 149 hotels, 27 attractions and 18 cultural sites. Saleh Mohamed Al Geziry, DCT Abu Dhabi's Director General for Tourism, said: "Every memorable visitor experience begins with the people who welcome our guests. The Visitor Experience Academy equips thousands of tourism frontliners with the knowledge, skills and confidence to consistently deliver the warm hospitality, service excellence and cultural understanding that define Abu Dhabi." This is the first time this initiative has cleared the bar for this dossier series, despite the Guidelines having first surfaced in searches on 19 Sep. It sits alongside DCT Abu Dhabi's inaugural Visitor Experience Awards (announced 17 Aug), which invited 400+ tourism organisations to compete across eight categories, with 24 winners due at a gala later in 2026, together forming a three-part government CX programme (standards, training, recognition) that MECSC members in tourism, hospitality and destination management can benchmark against.

CMSWire opens 2027 IMPACT Awards with new agentic-AI categories

AI & techMarket data23 Sep 2026 · in window

CMSWire opened submissions for its sixth annual IMPACT Awards, adding three new categories for the 2027 cycle: agentic AI customer-service strategies, AI applications to customer data and personalization, and AI-powered self-service capabilities, alongside existing categories spanning contact centres, conversational AI/chatbots and digital-experience analytics. Vendor early-entry closes 30 September 2026, the general submission deadline is 20 November 2026, and winners are announced in spring 2027. Submissions are reviewed by a pool of more than 140 vetted industry judges against measurable outcomes and supporting evidence, with winners receiving feature coverage, digital badges and trophies. Not a GCC-specific story, but a directly relevant one: it gives Middle East contact-centre and retail AI teams already investing in agentic AI (see the Dell/UAE AI Office item above, and mazeed's "Matha" agent logged in the 24 Sep dossier) a named, globally-judged benchmark to enter and cite in board-level AI-ROI conversations.

UAE AI Office partners with Dell Technologies to scale agentic AI in government services

AI & techUAEAI / tech21 Sep 2026 · recent

The Artificial Intelligence Office in the UAE Government, led by Dr. Abdulrahman Al Mahmoud, signed a strategic collaboration with Dell Technologies to advance agentic AI in government operations and services. The partnership will focus on developing prototypes and implementation-ready AI solutions, run specialised agentic-AI training sessions for government entities, and introduce federal and local teams to Dell's AI Factory ecosystem for building and deploying AI models. The announcement followed a delegation of more than 50 chief AI officers from federal and local government entities, organised by the AI Office and the Dubai Centre for Artificial Intelligence, who toured Dell's US operations. Dr. Al Mahmoud said cooperation with global technology companies is "a key element in accelerating the exchange of knowledge and expertise," while Dell's Walid Yehia noted that "the real impact of these technologies lies in the ability to transform ambitions and ideas into practical, scalable applications." This lands against the backdrop of the UAE's public target, flagged in earlier dossier cycles, of routing 50% of government services through agentic AI within two years. A vendor-and-enablement deal of this scale is a concrete step toward that target, and worth watching for the first named public-service use case to go live under it.
