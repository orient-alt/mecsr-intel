Dubai triples "A Dubai Invite" capacity after ~20,000 applications

CXUAECX17 Aug 2026 · in window

Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) has tripled the capacity of its "A Dubai Invite" programme after roughly 20,000 successful applications since its 20 July launch. The initiative lets Dubai residents, representing close to 200 nationalities, invite family and friends to visit the emirate, with guests unlocking vouchers and offers across participating hotels, restaurants, attractions and lifestyle destinations. Noor Al Geziry, AVP for Special Projects and MENA at DET, said the response demonstrates the role residents play as ambassadors for the city's experience; DET has worked with partners to expand the benefit pool as registrations approach the new, expanded cap. Registrations for guests arriving between 20 July and 31 October 2026 remain open, with offers valid through 31 December 2026.

Dubai Customs' AI-driven Smart Risk Engine credited in 2026 Competitiveness Report

AI & techUAEAI / tech17 Aug 2026 · in window

The UAE's 2026 Competitiveness Report, drawing on 30 international reports and 69 indicators, singles out Dubai Customs' shift to an AI-enabled operating model as a driver of the emirate's global competitiveness gains. Its "Smart Risk Engine" applies data analytics and AI to assess shipments and direct inspections more precisely, working alongside the "Advance Cargo Information" initiative to analyse shipment data before cargo reaches port. Director General Dr Abdulla Busenad said the transformation spans "the strategic use of data and artificial intelligence" alongside "an enhanced customer experience." The report credits the approach with a 12.5-point jump in the customs security pillar (37.5% to 50%) and continued high marks on trade facilitation (91.5%) and support services (100%, second year running); the "Mirsal" platform is cited for accelerating responses to customer inquiries.

CBD becomes first UAE bank to enable cross-bank payments via its mobile app

AI & techUAEAI / tech17 Aug 2026 · in window

Commercial Bank of Dubai (CBD) has become the first bank in the UAE to let customers initiate payments from accounts held at other banks directly through its mobile app, following its approval as a Third-Party Provider under the Central Bank of the UAE's Open Finance Initiative. Building on its December 2025 live activation of Open Finance, CBD customers can now view balances and initiate payments across banks from a single app, with CEO Dr Bernd van Linder and Chief Digital Officer Vladislavs Mironovs both framing it as a step toward more connected, data-driven customer experiences, with further Open Finance services planned later in 2026.
