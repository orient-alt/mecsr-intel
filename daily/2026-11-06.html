Emaar's mall and retail leasing revenue rises 10% to Dh5.2bn in nine months at about 98% occupancy, as hotel occupancy falls to 63%

DestinationsUAE · DubaiFinancial5 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Emaar Properties' revenue rose 13% to Dh37.5 billion in the first nine months of 2026, with property sales of Dh34.7 billion and a revenue backlog of Dh158.8 billion at the end of September, up 6%. Revenue from shopping malls, retail and commercial leasing rose 10% to Dh5.2 billion, with occupancy of about 98%, while average hotel occupancy fell to 63% from 80% a year earlier.

A day earlier Emaar proposed a cash offer of Dh15 a share for the Emaar Development shares it does not already own. Gulf News says the offer is still subject to board approval, while The National reports that the board has approved it and that Emaar plans to delist the unit.

Why it matters: The owner of The Dubai Mall grew leasing revenue at near-full occupancy while hotel occupancy fell sharply, a Q3 benchmark for Dubai mall landlords.

Majid Al Futtaim brings its SHARE rewards programme to Carrefour's 35 stores in Kenya

MembersKenya · NairobiLaunch5 Nov 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: High

Majid Al Futtaim launched its SHARE rewards programme in Kenya on 5 Nov inside the existing Carrefour app, with points earned on in-store and online Carrefour purchases at a base rate of 0.25%. About 1.4 million members of Carrefour's MyClub programme move to SHARE automatically once they accept the updated terms and set their marketing preferences; Carrefour has 35 stores in Kenya, mostly in Nairobi. Launch offers include up to 4,500 welcome points, double base points on SHARE Mondays and 20% back in points on eligible birthday purchases. SHARE already runs in the UAE, Egypt, Saudi Arabia and Oman and has more than 15 million members.

Why it matters: A member is moving a large loyalty base onto its regional platform inside a retailer's app, with fresh consent, a live case for MECSC's loyalty and CX content.

1

Dubai's RTA will pilot nol-Jaywan cards, joining the nol transit card to the UAE's national Jaywan payment scheme, from November

CXUAE · DubaiLaunch4 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority has signed an agreement with Al Etihad Payments, a subsidiary of the Central Bank of the UAE, to introduce new-generation nol cards in phases that combine the nol transport system with Jaywan, the UAE's national card payment scheme. A pilot starts in November 2026 on RTA public transport, including taxis and public parking; cards are activated and managed on Android and iOS phones, and wider use is planned across public and private transport and online booking platforms in the UAE. Cardholders get access to more than 120 promotional offers and discounts. RTA has not given a date for general availability, named the businesses that will accept the cards or said whether existing nol cards must be replaced.

Why it matters: A national-scheme payment card with built-in offers, carried by Dubai's daily commuters, could become a payment and promotions channel for malls and retailers once merchant acceptance is defined.

Khaleej Times · 4 Nov 2026 ↗BArabian Business · 6 Nov 2026 ↗A

1

Instacart will wind down its Caper smart-cart business by mid-2027 and move in-store work to software and AI

AI & techGlobalClosure27 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

Instacart said on 26 Oct that it is winding down its Caper Cart smart-cart business, to be completed by the end of Q2 2027, because 'retailer adoption has not scaled quickly enough to justify our continued investment'. Its next in-store phase centres on software, data and AI that report on store performance, including a live view of stores for grocers and consumer brands and Arpalus, an AI tool bought this summer that lets staff record product levels with a smartphone. The decision, disclosed in a company update and a regulatory filing, came days before DoorDash agreed to buy Instacart (see 2 Nov).

Why it matters: A leading smart-cart provider is retreating to software, a signal for GCC grocers and mall operators weighing checkout hardware against store-wide data and AI.

Grocery Dive · 27 Oct 2026 ↗B
