"The Grand" confirmed for 19 August opening in Nad Al Sheba, Dubai

DestinationsUAE29 Jul 2026 · in window

What's On Dubai confirmed that The Grand, a new neighbourhood mall in the Nad Al Sheba residential district, will welcome its first visitors on 19 August 2026, opening in stages. The first phase is anchored by Waitrose alongside MMI, Maple Bear Nursery, Al Jaber Optical and Fixperts, with homegrown dining brands (Tashas, Kokoro, Blu Pizzeria and others) following from early September, and health/wellness tenants (Fitcode, American Hospital) planned for early 2027. MEMBER NEWS BAHRAIN Seef Properties reports H1 2026 net profit of BD 1.9m, down 29.7% YoY Financial 28 Jul 2026

MembersBahrainFinancial28 Jul 2026 · in windowMECSR member

Company: Seef Properties (Priority 1 member, Manama). Seef Properties announced financial results for the six-month period ending 30 June 2026: net profit attributable to shareholders of BD 1.9 million, down from BD 2.7 million in H1 2025 (-29.7%); operating profit of BD 5.0 million (down 14.0%) on revenue of BD 6.6 million (down 12.9%). Earnings per share fell to 4.2 Bahraini fils from 5.9 fils. This is distinct from, and updates, Seef's ongoing GFH voluntary-offer discussions and contractor-appointment news already carried in the 26 July report.

MEMBER NEWS UAE Aldar Properties reports H1 2026 net profit up 18% to AED 4.9bn Financial 29 Jul 2026

MembersUAEFinancial29 Jul 2026 · in windowMECSR member

Company: Aldar Properties (Priority 1 member, Abu Dhabi). Aldar announced H1 2026 results: net profit after tax up 18% year-on-year to AED 4.9 billion, on revenue exceeding AED 16 billion; earnings per share grew 17% to AED 0.53. Group development sales reached AED 12.1 billion in H1, a measured pace of new UAE launches given regional market conditions, with three successful project launches in Q2. Development backlog stood at AED 71.6 billion at end-June (AED 59.9bn in the UAE), supporting revenue recognition over the next 2–3 years. UAE sales to overseas/expat buyers reached AED 7.6 billion (80% of total UAE sales), and international arms SODIC and London Square grew H1 sales contributions 171% and 236% respectively. The results commentary references Aldar's previously-announced retail-adjacent projects, the AED 100bn Marsa Al Saadiyat waterfront masterplan and the AED 6bn Yas Island branded-residences scheme, both already captured in this series (22 Jul and 10 Jul reports respectively); today's finding is the H1 financial results themselves, not a new project announcement.

UAE retail moves agentic AI from pilot to production: and flags a "shadow AI" risk

AI & techUAEAI / tech27 Jul 2026 · in window

Computer Weekly reports that UAE retailers are increasingly deploying agentic AI across pricing, inventory and merchandising as operations spread across physical stores, marketplaces and regional supply chains. Per Sulaiman Yusuf, UiPath's MEA regional VP, agents can now evaluate price elasticity, competitor activity and inventory in real time and either recommend or automatically execute pricing/replenishment decisions within governance thresholds. The piece also warns of an emerging "shadow AI" governance risk, echoing shadow-IT concerns from the cloud era, where organisations lose visibility into which AI agents can access customer data or take autonomous action, a relevant caution for GCC retailers and banks scaling agentic deployments.
