Cenomi keeps Westfield Riyadh on target to start operating in Q4 2026, while Westfield Jeddah's grand opening is still undated

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · Riyadh; JeddahStatus update12 Nov 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: High

In an interview Argaam published on 12 Nov, Cenomi Centers chief executive Alison Rehill-Erguven said Westfield Riyadh's structural works are complete, pre-leasing has approached 95% and the mall is targeted to enter its operational phase in Q4 2026. Westfield Jeddah, in its operational phase since July 2026, generated SAR 20.4 million of revenue in Q3 and is close to 97% pre-leased, but she gave no grand-opening date, saying the focus is on completing tenant fit-outs and opening stores and that pre-leasing does not necessarily reflect the occupancy of stores already trading. Footfall across the portfolio rose 20.7% to 39 million visitors in Q3 and like-for-like occupancy was 91.8%; these figures agree with Mubasher's 9 Nov copy of Cenomi's Q3 disclosure.

Why it matters: Two of the largest malls in the Saudi pipeline: Riyadh's Q4 2026 target still holds seven weeks before year-end, while Jeddah's grand opening, guided for Q4 2026, has no date yet.

Landmark Group alerts some Shukran members after unauthorised access to customer information

CXUAECX11 Nov 2026 · recentMECSR memberConfidence: Medium

Landmark Group messaged some members of its Shukran loyalty programme on WhatsApp late on Tuesday 10 Nov after identifying unauthorised access to some of their personal information, including names, contact details and dates of birth, Gulf News reported on 11 Nov. The company says payment card details and passwords were not affected, nor were Shukran points, balances and wallets, and that it acted immediately to contain the incident, launched an investigation, informed the relevant regulatory and law enforcement authorities and contacted affected customers directly. It has not said how many members or which markets are affected. Members were told to be wary of unexpected calls and messages; Shukran says it asks for one-time passwords only at its store counters or in the Shukran app.

Why it matters: A data incident at one of the region's largest retail loyalty programmes, and a live case of breach communication for MECSC's customer-service and loyalty members.

Creators HQ and YouTube will train more than 1,000 creators to sell through YouTube Shopping in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Egypt

AI & techUAE · DubaiLaunch11 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Dubai's Creators HQ and YouTube have launched a regional programme to train more than 1,000 creators across the Middle East and North Africa over the next two years through YouTube Academy masterclasses, so they can earn commission by tagging products in their videos. The YouTube Shopping affiliate programme is open to eligible creators in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Egypt, with Amazon as its first affiliate partner and more merchants expected; it uses Google AI to identify products in videos and suggest tags, and viewers watching on television can buy by scanning an on-screen QR code. A YouTube Shopping experience will feature at the 1 Billion Followers Summit in Dubai on 8 to 10 January 2027.

Why it matters: AI-assisted creator commerce is opening a sales channel in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Egypt that retailers and mall brands can join as merchants.
