Qatar Airways resumes Bahrain, Kuwait and Erbil flights from 8 August

CXBahrainCX6 Aug 2026 · in window

Qatar Airways announced it will restore passenger services to Bahrain (BAH), Kuwait (KWI) and Erbil (EBL) from 8 August, ending a suspension that ran "until and including August 7" as Gulf carriers adjusted to airspace restrictions triggered by regional hostilities. Passengers are directed to the airline's website for updates. For MECSC members, this is the first concrete step in normalising cross-Gulf service levels after weeks of cancellations and rebooking pressure on contact centres across GCC aviation.
