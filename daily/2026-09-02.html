Dubai Police's AI-enhanced protection platform cuts response times, response rate up 200%

CXUAECX20 Jul 2026 · recent

Dubai Police upgraded its Child Protection and Women's Protection reporting platform with AI and voice-recording technology, adding an "Instant Assistant" feature designed to speed response while preserving reporter privacy through encryption. The upgrade drove a 200% increase in response rates to reports involving women. The smart app and Women's Protection platform together accounted for 95% of all women's reports and complaints in 2025, while telephone reporting remains the primary channel for child-protection cases (60% of submissions); emergency cases receive immediate intervention, with non-urgent cases processed within two working days. This is a useful data point for MECSC members on how a public-safety service, not a typical "CX" function, is treating response-rate improvement and channel mix as measurable service-experience outcomes, with privacy-by-design built into the AI layer rather than bolted on afterward.

Merkle MENA and Adobe launch "Experience Concierge for Retail" for the region

AI & techMENAAI / tech24 Aug 2026 · recent

Merkle MENA, working with Adobe, has built an "Experience Concierge for Retail", a multi-agent system designed to orchestrate entire customer journeys rather than isolated chatbot interactions, explicitly aimed at MENA retailers' fragmented legacy-plus-modern tech stacks and multilingual, multi-payment-method customer base. The system targets 15–30% conversion lifts, 10–25% average-order-value growth and 20–40% service-cost reductions. It sits inside a broader global shift the article frames with Deloitte's 2026 outlook (nearly 90% of retail executives expect AI to outpace search engines) and industry estimates that AI agents could handle 15–25% of global e-commerce sales by 2030; roughly 40% of consumers already use AI for product discovery, and 77% say conversational experiences make shopping easier. For MECSC members, this is a concrete, regionally-built example of the "agentic commerce" trend rather than a global vendor case study, worth watching for named GCC retailer adopters in coming months.

ruya takes agentic AI live in production, customer-facing assistants next

AI & techUAEAI / tech18 Aug 2026 · recent

UAE digital bank ruya confirms three agentic-AI use cases are now live in production, covering business-account onboarding and transaction processing, including document analysis, verification checks and case summarisation, moving the bank beyond the pilot/roadmap stage flagged in earlier dossiers (its Magure partnership, logged 10–14 Aug). The AI infrastructure runs inside ruya's private cloud so customer data stays within the bank's own systems, with governance controls, audit trails and human oversight built in to align with UAE Central Bank expectations. The CEO framed it plainly: "We didn't set out to run AI projects, we set out to build a bank where intelligence is part of the foundation." Collections management, compliance support, an internal employee assistant (RuyaAI) and, most relevant to MECSC members, customer-facing AI assistants and personalization are next on the roadmap. The practical read: this is one of the clearer regional examples of agentic AI moving from announcement to measurable production use inside a regulated environment, worth tracking as the customer-facing layer arrives.
