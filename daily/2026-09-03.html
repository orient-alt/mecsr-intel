Abu Dhabi's world-first AI judicial platform begins rollout this month

CXUAECX28 Jul 2026 · recent

Abu Dhabi's judicial department confirmed its integrated AI judicial platform, described as the first fully integrated system of its kind, starts an 18-month phased rollout beginning September 2026. The system applies AI across litigation stages, including preliminary case analysis and administrative workflow, aiming to accelerate case resolution and improve judicial accuracy, with all final decisions remaining under human judicial oversight. The rollout lands within the UAE's wider push (per President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed's April 2026 directive) to convert 50% of government services and operations to autonomous AI within two years. The practical read for MECSC members: a concrete example of AI doing the case-analysis "back office" work while a human keeps the final call, a governance pattern worth studying for any regulated service function considering agentic AI.

UAE Cabinet launches AI Advisor System with 32 specialised advisors

AI & techUAEAI / tech2 Sep 2026 · in window

The UAE Cabinet approved a new AI Advisor System comprising 32 specialised AI advisors tasked with analysing policy and legislation, assessing impact, reviewing global best practice, and issuing recommendations, operating continuously to track how government decisions are executed. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum linked the launch to a broader push toward "faster, more precise decision-making," and the same Cabinet session approved AI training for 22,000 teachers plus a nationwide AI school curriculum. The announcement lands almost exactly at the two-year mark set by the President's April 2026 directive to convert 50% of government services to autonomous AI. Not customer-facing on its own, but a strong signal of how quickly agentic AI is moving from pilot into standing government infrastructure in the UAE, relevant context for members benchmarking public-sector AI maturity, and a plausible precursor to citizen-facing recommendation or self-service tools down the line.
