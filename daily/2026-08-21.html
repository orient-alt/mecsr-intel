LuLu Hypermarket turns Bahrain stores into festive destinations for "Onam with LuLu 2026"

DestinationsBahrain20 Aug 2026 · in window

LuLu Hypermarket has launched "Onam with LuLu 2026" across all its Bahrain stores, running until 26 August, bringing customers themed food, fashion and market experiences built around Kerala's Onam harvest festival. The celebrations were inaugurated at LuLu's Dana Mall store in the presence of Rajiv Kumar Mishra, Chargé d'Affaires at the Embassy of India to Bahrain. Stores have been reconfigured into "Onam Fresh Markets" with fresh produce, flowers and festive essentials, alongside a Payasam Mela (nine dessert varieties, including a "Baklava Payasam" fusion dish) and a Pickle Mela of traditional pickles. The centrepiece is LuLu's Traditional Onam Sadhya, a 24-dish set meal offered at a "Happiness Price" of BD 2.990, which director Juzer Rupawala described as "one of the most famous and eagerly awaited food experiences in Bahrain." A parallel ethnic-fashion promotion (BD 20 for BD 15, valid to 29 August) rounds out the activation. As a tenant-mix and in-store experience play rather than a property story, this sits squarely in MECSC's experience-angle remit, see mecsr-weekly-intel-report for any related property/development tracking.

Nesto opens first Qatar hypermarket in Al Gharrafa, Doha

DestinationsQatarNew property20 Aug 2026 · in window

Nesto Group (Western International Group) entered the Qatari market with the opening of its first hypermarket, located in Al Gharrafa, Doha, on the site of the former Passport Office building, the group's 152nd store worldwide. The store offers fresh produce, groceries, household essentials, fashion, electronics and lifestyle products, and was inaugurated in the presence of Qatar Chamber board member Mohammed bin Ahmed Al Obaidli, Sheikh Hamad bin Thani Al Thani, Western International Group Chairman K.P. Basheer and Nesto Group Managing Director Jamal Kunhiparambath. The company confirmed eight further outlets are in the pipeline in Qatar, with other coverage citing a QR2bn investment plan targeting up to 10 hypermarkets in the country by 2026, four of which are already under way.

Grant Thornton Bahrain: AI is changing cybersecurity risk: and customer-service continuity along with it

AI & techBahrainMarket data16 Aug 2026 · recent

Grant Thornton Bahrain has warned that artificial intelligence is transforming how businesses in the Kingdom operate while also changing the cybersecurity landscape around them. While AI gives organisations faster threat-detection capabilities, it also lets bad actors automate attacks with unprecedented scale and speed, according to senior partner Jatin Karia: "AI is fundamentally changing the cybersecurity landscape, creating both opportunities and new areas of risk." The firm flags that operational risk now extends beyond traditional IT failure, disruptions to cloud providers or vendor networks can "instantly freeze customer services and financial workflows", while autonomous AI agents introduce further exposure through prompt-injection attacks, AI-driven data exposure, identity breaches, and ungoverned "shadow AI" tools used by employees without authorisation. Technology advisory director Shams Khan said securing these systems "requires ongoing monitoring rather than a single assessment," while senior manager Vitesh Sharma called for a balance between innovation and governance, identity controls, data protection and human oversight. The piece is a useful governance-side counterpart to the many customer-facing AI/CX deployment stories this series otherwise tracks, a reminder that GCC organisations scaling agentic AI into service channels are also being urged to build resilience so those same channels don't become a single point of failure.
