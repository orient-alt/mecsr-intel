Ibn Battuta Mall, Dubai: 38 new outlets and the return of cinema

DestinationsUAEExpansion12 Oct 2026 · in window→ 2027 Directory: Update listing · Medium

Dubai Retail announced a redevelopment of its 2005-opened Ibn Battuta Mall taking in 38 new and upcoming retail, dining, entertainment and service outlets. Twenty-one have already opened and the remaining 17 are scheduled by the end of 2027. The headline addition is Roxy Cinemas, which restores a cinema offer the mall had lost, alongside the Hopa Loompa family entertainment centre, Zamania by Leo & Loona and Toy Station. New retail includes Jack & Jones, Anta, Tanishq, Magrabi, OWNDAYS, Mumuso and Chemist Warehouse; F&B additions include Five Guys, Marugame Udon, Caribou Coffee, Peet's Coffee and Ocean Basket. Dubai Retail puts the mall at more than 350 businesses and an average of roughly 1.5 million visits a month. Several outlets framed the announcement as a direct rebuttal to social-media claims that the mall was in decline.

Saadiyat Grove, Abu Dhabi: luxury brand line-up revealed ahead of Q4 2026 opening

DestinationsUAENew property12 Oct 2026 · in window→ 2027 Directory: Add to pipeline

Aldar's experiential luxury retail and fine-dining destination in the Saadiyat Cultural District had its tenant line-up revealed in first-look coverage on 12 October, ahead of a fourth-quarter 2026 opening. Confirmed maisons include Louis Vuitton, Dior, Cartier, Bvlgari, Van Cleef & Arpels, Fendi, Loewe, Tiffany & Co., Valentino, Chaumet, Audemars Piguet and Brunello Cucinelli. The scheme also brings Abu Dhabi its first Time Out Market, plus restaurants, cafés, wellness facilities, luxury hotels, branded residences (Louvre Abu Dhabi Residences, Baccarat Residences Saadiyat, The Arthouse) and premium office space. It sits between the Louvre Abu Dhabi, the forthcoming Guggenheim Abu Dhabi and the Zayed National Museum. The project itself was launched by Aldar on 18 September 2026 with a stated 100+ brands; the 12 October reporting is the brand-level reveal.

Skylight Mall, Al Khail Road, Dubai: 1.3m sq ft super-regional mall for 2027

DestinationsUAENew property6 Oct 2026 · recent→ 2027 Directory: Add to pipeline

Dubai Retail unveiled Skylight Mall, a super-regional destination on Al Khail Road with 1.3 million sq ft of gross leasable area and more than 360 stores, targeted to open in 2027. It is positioned to serve Jumeirah Village Triangle, Jumeirah Islands and The Springs alongside citywide catchment, combining retail with dining, entertainment and everyday services. Mall of the Emirates, Dubai: The Hangout precinct within the AED5bn transformation

DestinationsUAEExpansion1 Oct 2026 · recent→ 2027 Directory: Update listing · Low

Majid Al Futtaim unveiled The Hangout by Mall of the Emirates, an indoor-outdoor lifestyle and entertainment precinct forming part of the AED5 billion transformation of the mall. It carries four entertainment concepts, Activate (12 light-up rooms, 50+ challenges, opening October 2026), SOCO (2,700 sq m; bowling, simulators, AR darts, karaoke, Q4 2026), Explorers (2,500 sq m family play for ages 1–12, Q1 2027) and a reimagined Magic Planet (2027), plus 18 F&B experiences across 12 restaurants, four kiosks and two in-venue outlets, including Fatt Pundit, Apollo, Crystal Jade Noodle Bar and Public Pizzeria. It introduces the mall's first dedicated outdoor F&B courtyard with operable façades and a climate-controlled pedestrian link to the New Covent Garden Theatre and Fashion Dome. This item sat on the watch list from the 6 and 9 October runs; it is now published as recent background with the developer's own release as the primary source.
