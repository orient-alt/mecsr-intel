Bahrain plans Six Flags theme park in bid to create new GCC entertainment hub 13 Jul 2026, 17:12 GST

DestinationsBahrain13 Jul 2026 · in window

A proposal by Capital Trustees Board member Dr Bashar Ahmedi, backed by the board's general secretariat and Parliament's financial and economic affairs committee, has been referred to the Sustainable Development Minister and EDB CEO Noor Al Khulaif. The concept deliberately avoids replicating Six Flags Qiddiya City (Saudi Arabia, recently opened), targeting original record-breaking attractions. Candidate sites include coastal zones between Busaiteen–Manama, Manama–Salman Town, Askar–Jaw and near Bilaj Al Jazayer. MP Ahmed Al Salloom highlighted spill-over activity across "tourism, hospitality, retail, transport and investment", a significant potential anchor for Bahrain's retail and F&B market. Caveat: proposal stage only; depends on Six Flags Corp's expansion appetite (currently divesting North American parks).

LabelVie to be absorbed by parent Retail Holding in all-stock merger: effective Aug 2026 c. 23 May 2026 URL-dated, on-page date unverified

DestinationsMorocco23 May 2026 · recent

Morocco's leading food retailer LabelVie S.A. (411 stores, 10,300 staff, Carrefour's Moroccan partner) will be absorbed by its unlisted parent Retail Holding in an all-stock merger expected to take effect in August 2026, with Retail Holding to be listed on the Casablanca Stock Exchange. The combined entity targets doubling revenue to MAD 47bn (~$5.08bn) by 2030 with continental expansion ambitions. Structural consolidation in North Africa's most advanced organised-retail market, relevant to any member scouting Moroccan anchor tenants. First capture in this report series.

MEMBER NEWS Jabal Omar to offer ~400 serviced residential units for sale under new property rules Financial 28 Jun 2026

MembersSaudi ArabiaFinancial28 Jun 2026 · recentMECSR member

Jabal Omar Development Company (Priority 1 member) plans to offer around 400 serviced residential units for sale in its Makkah masterplan, leveraging the Kingdom's newly approved foreign-ownership property rules (Jabal Omar is inside an approved Makkah zone, ownership limited to Muslims). This is a direct monetisation of the regulatory change captured in earlier reports and complements the SAR 2bn Murabaha refinancing signed 18 June, together signalling an assets-to-cash strategy ahead of further retail/hospitality phases.
