Dubai Development Launches "Walk'n" Mixed-Use Project, West Cairo

DestinationsEgypt5 Jul 2026 · in window

Dubai Development, a Saudi-linked developer (investment partner: Rsoukh), not to be confused with the UAE's Dubai Development Authority, launched Walk'n , a roughly 10-feddan mixed-use project in 6th of October City combining retail, offices, medical clinics, serviced apartments and entertainment. The company confirmed further 2026 launches including The Address Mall, Dubai Mall (West Cairo) and Strip 13 Mall, plus planned expansion into East Cairo and the North Coast, signalling a new, fairly aggressive retail-development entrant in the Cairo market.

First Mall Opens in Tripoli: Libya's Largest Shopping Complex

DestinationsLibya26 Jun 2026 · recent

First Mall , developed by Al-Sahl Holding Group, opened in Tripoli after a stop-start construction history of more than 15 years. The 136,000 sqm complex (on a 6-hectare site) houses 220+ retail outlets, administrative offices, bank branches, medical clinics and event halls. Prime Minister Abdel Hamid Aldabaiba attended the opening ceremony. This is a distinct property from Zumurrud Mall, Tripoli (already tracked in MECSR reporting), the two should not be conflated in the directory.

Morocco Mall Marrakech Opening Delayed to Spring 2027

DestinationsMorocco22 Apr 2026 · recent

Aksal Group (led by Saloua Idrissi Akhannouch) has pushed back the opening of the ~130,000 sqm Morocco Mall Marrakech from summer 2026 to spring 2027. Shell/construction work is reported essentially complete; the delay is described as a deliberate decision to secure a stronger pre-leasing occupancy rate amid a fragile Moroccan retail climate (brand exits, spending pressure), rather than a technical setback. This reverses the "on track for summer 2026" narrative and is a genuine status change worth flagging.

Mall of Al Ain: Threefold Redevelopment & Expansion Announced

DestinationsUAE2 Jul 2026 · recent

MAIR Group PJSC, via its commercial real estate arm Makani Real Estate , announced a major redevelopment and expansion of Mall of Al Ain ahead of its 25th anniversary, its first major investment in the asset since acquiring it in 2024. Plans include a full façade transformation and an enlarged, relocated ice rink expected to be one of the largest in Al Ain, plus hotel services. Construction begins Q4 2026; the mall stays open throughout. Disclosed via an Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange (ADX) filing, published one day before this run's strict coverage window and missed by the 3 July report.
