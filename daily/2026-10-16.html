Oman to set up duty-free markets at three land border crossings with the UAE and Saudi Arabia

DestinationsOman · Al Wajajah, Khatmat Malaha and Rub Al Khali crossingsPolicy13 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium→ 2027 Directory: Article idea

Lt Gen Hassan bin Mohsin Al Shuraiqi, Inspector General of Police and Customs, issued Decision No 145/2026 establishing duty-free markets at the Al Wajajah and Khatmat Malaha crossings on the UAE border and the Rub Al Khali crossing on the Saudi border, Muscat Daily reported on 13 October 2026. Goods will be sold exempt from customs duties and taxes, subject to applicable regulations, to boost activity at the land ports, support tourism and logistics and give travellers more shopping options. No start date, operator, size or investment was given.

Why it matters: Duty free at road borders is a new travel-retail format in the GCC that could capture spend from UAE and Saudi visitors and open tenders for retail operators.

Qatar's Ministry of Municipality reports 89.1% call-centre satisfaction and 891,218 digital transactions

CXQatar · DohaCX11 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

From 1 January to 31 August 2026, Qatar's Ministry of Municipality completed 891,218 transactions through digital channels, including 461,459 on its website and 191,040 in the Oun app. Its Customer Service Department and Unified Call Center provided 238,719 services and received 188,280 calls, with satisfaction of 89.1% for the call centre and 83.82% for the customer service offices. The Peninsula (11 October) and Al Raya (10 October) report identical figures.

Why it matters: Published satisfaction scores for a Gulf ministry's call centre and service offices give MECSC a public-sector benchmark and a possible Qatar case study.

AI & retail technology

3

OpenAI adds virtual try-on and saved favourites to ChatGPT shopping

AI & techGlobalLaunch2 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

OpenAI has added a 'Try On' button to supported clothing and accessory listings in ChatGPT shopping: users take or upload a selfie and ChatGPT Images generates a picture of them wearing the item, while a Favorites feature saves products to the ChatGPT Library. TechRepublic reported the launch on 2 October 2026 for mobile and web, and Gulf News said the global rollout began on 1 October. The preview cannot confirm fit or size, and reference photos are kept for future try-ons and can be removed in settings.

Why it matters: Try-on now sits inside a general-purpose assistant that shoppers use before they reach a mall or a brand's own app, the same feature Gulf fashion retailers are adding in store.

TechRepublic · 2 Oct 2026 ↗BGulf News · 2 Oct 2026 ↗B

Zain Kuwait backs ETLAQ's 'Agentic AI Experience' to help Kuwaiti firms deploy AI agents

AI & techKuwait · Kuwait CityLaunch7 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

Zain Kuwait has announced a strategic partnership with ETLAQ, which created and runs The Agentic AI Experience, a programme that helps business owners and executives design and deploy teams of AI agents with defined responsibilities, permissions and human approval points. Its masterclass runs at GUST from 2 November to 2 December 2026, alongside mentoring, talks and a showcase. Kuwait's ICT regulator CITRA is the digital trust partner, and its chairman said permissions and responsibility for agents' actions must be clearly defined.

Why it matters: Kuwait's telecom leader and its ICT regulator are setting expectations for how businesses run AI agents, which makes them useful partners and speakers for MECSR's Kuwait members and AI content.

Kuwait Times · 7 Oct 2026 ↗B

Etisalat UAE signs a multi-year DataRobot deal to build and govern an agentic AI workforce

AI & techUAEDeal / JV14 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Etisalat UAE has extended its partnership with DataRobot through a multi-year agreement to build, operate and govern an agentic AI workforce across its business units, using the DataRobot Agent Workforce Platform within its sovereign AI ecosystem. Predictive, generative and agentic AI will run under one governance framework, with sensitive data and AI workloads kept on Etisalat UAE's own infrastructure. The companies have worked together since 2022 on customer value management, including churn prevention, upsell and cross-sell targeting and credit risk; no value was disclosed.

Why it matters: A major Gulf consumer brand is putting AI agents into customer-facing work under central governance, the same model the UAE now requires of federal AI agents.
