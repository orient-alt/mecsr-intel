Cenomi Centers signs a three-year, SAR 104.3m facilities management contract for Westfield Jeddah

MembersSaudi Arabia · JeddahDeal / JV26 Oct 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: Medium

Cenomi Centers signed a three-year integrated facilities management contract for Westfield Jeddah with DAAM Facilities Management, at an estimated SAR 104.3 million excluding VAT: a fixed annual base of SAR 28.65 million plus specialist services at cost with a 5% management fee, estimated at SAR 6.12 million a year. The chairman has an indirect interest in DAAM, so it is a related-party transaction; he abstained from the board vote, and the contract goes to the general assembly each year.

Cenomi expects the cost to show in its results from Q4 2026. The figures are as reported by Argaam from the company's Tadawul disclosure, which gives no opening date.

Why it matters: Running costs for Westfield Jeddah start in Q4 2026, when Cenomi plans the mall's grand opening, in the host city of RCM Jeddah (7 to 8 Dec).

Decathlon Türkiye rebuilds its online store around an analysis of customer feedback

CXTürkiyeCX26 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Decathlon Türkiye has redesigned its online store across the whole journey, from first digital contact to after-sales support, starting from an analysis of customer feedback to find where shoppers ran into problems. The changes cover richer product content and personalised offers, site performance under heavy traffic, stock information kept in step across product, category and cart pages, and simpler payment, delivery, order tracking and returns. No user or sales figures were given.

Why it matters: A current example of a large sports retailer fixing its digital journey from customer feedback, useful for MECSC's omnichannel service content.

AI & retail technology

2

Dubai Chambers launches Chamber AI Hub, a marketplace of 22 ready-made AI agents for companies

AI & techUAE · DubaiLaunch26 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Dubai Chambers has launched Chamber AI Hub, which it calls the world's first chamber-led agentic AI marketplace, with 22 agents across compliance, procurement, operations, finance, sales, HR and logistics, for tasks such as supplier discovery, contract review, invoice matching, lead scoring and IT help-desk requests; 100 agents are planned over two years. Companies can test agents in a sandbox with free starter credits and then pay by usage; data is hosted in the UAE and kept separate for each company, and agents work in Arabic and English.

The programme aims to reach more than 295,000 private-sector companies, with training for 14,000 companies from the Chamber's business groups and councils. No retail or customer-service agents are listed yet.

Why it matters: Dubai retailers and mall operators can trial vetted, UAE-hosted agents for back-office work at low cost, and the move to 100 agents is a chance to ask for retail and customer-service ones.

Saudia Cargo puts AI agents on freight quotes, and an agent-to-agent tourism corridor links Red Sea Global and AlUla

AI & techSaudi ArabiaAI / tech22 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

Arab News reports that Saudia Cargo's AI agents now triage emailed freight-rate requests in several languages, calculate multi-leg options and prepare quotes in seconds, passing non-standard or high-value cases to human sales teams, with every action logged and auditable. Separately, Globant and Tourise have built an agentic protocol for tourism whose first live partners are Red Sea Global and AlUla: destination agents share only the data needed for each interaction, while rule-based systems keep bookings, payments and compliance, and international airlines are the next partners. No volumes or figures were given.

Why it matters: Saudi examples of customer-facing agents with human hand-offs, data minimisation and audit logs, the controls MECSC can point members to; single source so far.
