Dubai Shopping Festival 2026-27 opens with Rashid Al Majed and Steve Aoki and adds a light show at JBR; mall offers still to come

DestinationsUAE · DubaiEvent10 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

The 32nd Dubai Shopping Festival, organised by Dubai Festivals and Retail Establishment (DFRE), runs for 45 days from 4 Dec 2026 to 17 Jan 2027. Rashid Al Majed (4 Dec) and Steve Aoki (5 Dec) headline the opening weekend at Coca-Cola Arena, and a free candlelight concert at DSF x Hatta Wadi Hub on 2 Dec marks Eid Al Etihad. A new DSF Light Show runs on the bay between The Beach JBR and Bluewaters Island from 5 Dec, 7.30pm to 10.30pm; Khaleej Times says it continues nightly to 17 Jan and includes drone shows. Shopping promotions and prize draws at participating malls and destinations are still to be announced.

Why it matters: DSF is the main seasonal footfall driver for Dubai's malls, and the retail promotions that matter most to them have not yet been published.

Nakheel unveils Palm Springs, a 3.8 million sq m community for about 50,000 residents next to Palm Jebel Ali, with retail destinations planned

DestinationsUAE · DubaiPipeline10 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Nakheel, part of Dubai Holding Real Estate, has unveiled Palm Springs, a 3.8 million sq m masterplan next to Palm Jebel Ali in southern Dubai for about 50,000 residents in villas, townhouses and apartments. The plans include retail and leisure destinations, a Country Club and a Sports Club, about 593,000 sq m of open space and more than 35 km of cycling, jogging and walking routes. Nakheel did not give the value of the development or its timeline, and no retail area has been published.

Why it matters: A community of this size next to Palm Jebel Ali adds future retail demand in southern Dubai, and its retail destinations become a pipeline lead once Nakheel defines them.

AFG Retail, formerly Cenomi Retail, narrows its nine-month loss to SAR 233.1m as revenue rises 6% and Q3 turns an operating profit

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · RiyadhFinancial5 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

AFG Retail (AFG International Co., formerly Fawaz Abdulaziz Alhokair Co. and known until recently as Cenomi Retail) reported a nine-month 2026 net loss of SAR 233.1m against SAR 239.4m a year earlier, including a Q3 loss of SAR 99.2m against SAR 129.6m (Argaam, 5 Nov; Mubasher, 8 Nov). Nine-month revenue rose 6.3% to about SAR 3.9bn, and Q3 brought an operating profit of SAR 33.4m against an operating loss of SAR 35.2m a year earlier. Mubasher attributes the revenue growth to electronics and international markets, and the narrower Q3 loss to higher revenue and gross profit and lower selling and administrative costs.

Why it matters: AFG Retail runs franchised fashion and other brands in malls across Saudi Arabia, so its results bear on the health of a major tenant in members' Saudi centres.

Emirates Skywards and Majid Al Futtaim's SHARE let linked members convert points into miles and back

CXUAE · DubaiDeal / JV10 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Emirates Skywards and SHARE by Majid Al Futtaim announced a partnership on 10 Nov 2026 under which members who link their accounts in the SHARE or Skywards Everyday app can convert 2 SHARE points into 1 Skywards Mile, or 5 Skywards Miles into 1 SHARE point, in conversions of 2,000 to 150,000 points per transaction. Emirates says SHARE has more than 16 million members across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Oman and Kenya and covers more than 5,000 stores, brands and experiences in Majid Al Futtaim's portfolio, naming Carrefour, VOX Cinemas and Ski Dubai. The release gives no separate start date.

Why it matters: It links the rewards earned in Majid Al Futtaim's malls, Carrefour stores and leisure venues to an airline currency, a reference point for mall operators' own loyalty programmes and for MECSC's customer-experience work.

AI & retail technology

1

etisalat by e& starts selling Meta's AI glasses in the UAE, from Dh1,160

AI & techUAE · DubaiLaunch10 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

etisalat by e& said on 10 Nov 2026 that it now sells Meta's AI glasses in the UAE from Dh1,160: the Meta Glasses, Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 and Gen 3, and Oakley Meta HSTN. The company says it is the first telecom company in the Middle East and North Africa to offer them, and Gulf News says they are sold in etisalat stores with SmartPay instalment plans. The National notes that in the UAE, owning smart glasses is permitted, but using their recording features without consent can lead to penalties.

Why it matters: Camera-equipped AI glasses sold through a mass-market telecom channel bring hands-free AI search and recording into malls and stores, which matters for shopper journeys and for malls' privacy and filming rules.
