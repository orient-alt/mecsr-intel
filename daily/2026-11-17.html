DestinationsSaudi Arabia · JeddahPipeline16 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

London-listed Dar Global has announced plans for a mixed-use development in Jeddah with an estimated gross development value of about SAR 1 billion (USD 267 million), on land acquired for about USD 56 million, which will bring an unnamed international luxury brand to the city through a co-branded residential concept (Dar Global release and The National, both 16 Nov). Knowledge Economic City expects two Madinah shopping centres to start trading in 2027, one by the end of Q2

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · MadinahPipeline13 Nov 2026 · recentMECSR memberConfidence: Medium

Knowledge Economic City's chief financial officer, Fahd Al-Zumai, told Asharq Bloomberg that two of its shopping centres, one on King Abdulaziz Road and one next to the train station, should start operating in 2027, that one is being prepared to open by the end of Q2 2027, and that leases have been signed for a major part of the commercial space (Argaam, 13 Nov). Majid Al Futtaim and DP World plan a refrigerated route for Egyptian produce to MAF's UAE stores

BrandsEgypt · CairoDeal / JV16 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Majid Al Futtaim Holding and DP World signed a memorandum of understanding at Mall of Egypt in Cairo to increase container shipments from Egypt to the UAE, starting with fresh fruit and vegetables: produce from MAF's Egyptian suppliers would be consolidated at DP World's temperature-controlled facilities in Sokhna, shipped from Sokhna Port and moved under refrigeration to Jebel Ali for distribution across MAF's retail network (Gulf News, 16 Nov). The partners gave no shipment target or start date and plan to add product categories and markets later. MAF Holding chief executive Ahmed Galal Ismail, who signed with DP World GCC chief executive Ahmad Yousef Al-Hassan, said the same day that the group has invested about USD 2.7 billion in Egypt (Amwal Al Ghad, over 30 years; Asharq Business says more than USD 2.7 billion since it entered the market).

Why it matters: MAF owns Mall of Egypt and the City Centre malls and runs Carrefour, so its own cold chain from Egypt bears on fresh-food supply at the grocery anchors of its UAE malls.

Al Othaim Markets posts a SAR 39.26m Q3 loss, blaming lower sales after an ERP system rollout

BrandsSaudi Arabia · RiyadhFinancial10 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Abdullah Al Othaim Markets reported a net loss of SAR 39.26 million for Q3 2026, against a net profit of SAR 17.48 million a year earlier, and a nine-month net loss of SAR 92.73 million, against a profit of SAR 134.98 million (Argaam and Al Eqtisadiah, both 10 Nov). The Q3 loss is narrower than Q2's SAR 107.13 million. The company attributes the results to lower sales linked to operational and technical problems with its ERP system rollout and to changes in consumer behaviour and the market, plus higher inventory provisions and e-commerce costs.

Why it matters: Al Othaim is a large grocery anchor in Saudi community and neighbourhood centres, and its swing to losses after a systems rollout is a watch point for its landlords.

Commerce rolls out Shopping Agent, a brandable AI assistant for BigCommerce storefronts

AI & techGlobal · AustinLaunch16 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Commerce, the company behind BigCommerce, began rolling out Shopping Agent to eligible BigCommerce merchants on 16 Nov, ahead of the holiday selling season: a storefront AI assistant that merchants can customise with their own branding to guide shoppers. Conversational storefront search is now available on all paid BigCommerce plans, and a B2B Agent for purchase-order workflows is in early access. The release names no regions, saying only that the product will expand to more regions and verticals over time.

Why it matters: Retailer-owned AI shopping assistants are becoming a standard e-commerce platform feature, a way for Gulf retailers to keep shopping conversations on their own sites as outside agents spread.

etisalat launches AIO for businesses and targets 75% of UAE care calls deflected from human agents by 2031

AI & techUAE · DubaiLaunch16 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

etisalat unveiled etisalat AIO at its AI Innovation Day at Bluewaters in Dubai on 16 Nov: an ecosystem of what it calls 10 capability stacks, from AI data centres, compute and a token factory to edge AI, security, agentic AI and robotics, for businesses to run AI on sovereign infrastructure; no customers were named (Campaign Middle East, 16 Nov). The group's Capital Markets Day deck of 28 Oct sets 2031 targets for etisalat UAE of 75% of calls deflected from human agents, a 30-50% cut in average handling time and a 5-point NPS gain, and reports an NPS of +78 at launch for its AI voice agents. The deck also says its first conversational AI open to the public is live on its customer-care numbers.

Why it matters: A leading UAE telco now sells AI infrastructure and agents to businesses and has put numbers on AI in its own contact centres, a benchmark for MECSC's customer-service community.
