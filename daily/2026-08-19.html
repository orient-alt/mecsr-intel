The Galleria, Al Maryah Island: 7 new retail & dining brands announced

DestinationsUAEExpansion17 Aug 2026 · in window

Al Maryah Retail Company (which operates The Galleria as part of the Aldar–Mubadala Abu Dhabi retail joint venture) is adding six new stores and two new dining concepts. First Mall opens in Tripoli: Libya's largest shopping mall

DestinationsLibyaNew property27 Jun 2026 · recent

After more than 15 years of stop-start construction, First Mall officially opened in Tripoli in a ceremony attended by Prime Minister Abdel Hamid Aldabaiba. Developed by Al-Sahl Holding Group, the complex covers 6 hectares with over 136,000 sqm of roofed area and houses more than 220 retail outlets alongside administrative offices, bank branches, medical clinics and event/conference facilities. It is described by the developer as one of the largest and most important investment and commercial complexes in the country, and a signal of renewed private-sector confidence in the Libyan market. Libya is one of MECSR's most thinly represented markets (3 directory properties); this is a first-captured finding for the country in the daily series and merits directory follow-up.

Sharjah Airport achieves ACI Level 3 Customer Experience Accreditation

CXUAECX18 Aug 2026 · in window

Sharjah Airport has achieved Level 3 Airport Customer Experience Accreditation from Airports Council International (ACI), its highest rating since first joining the global evaluation programme in 2022 (Level 1), progressing to Level 2 in 2023 and renewing that rating in 2025. Ali Salim Al Midfa, Chairman of Sharjah Airport Authority, said the accreditation reflects the airport's commitment to making passenger satisfaction central to its operations, in line with its strategy to deliver an integrated travel experience "built on quality and excellence." To reach Level 3, the airport strengthened customer feedback tools to identify improvement areas and enable faster responses, and expanded collaboration with airlines, ground handlers and other stakeholders to ensure consistent service standards throughout the passenger journey. Passenger satisfaction reports are now shared regularly across airport departments, and the accreditation assessed how feedback is collected and used, coordination with service providers, and the airport's ability to deliver a seamless journey from arrival to departure.

Digital Government Authority's digital experience maturity index rises to 87.06%

CXSaudi ArabiaCX13 Aug 2026 · recent

Saudi Arabia's Digital Government Authority (DGA) has announced that its digital experience maturity index, one of the metrics the DGA uses to track how well government digital services perform for the people who actually use them, rose to 87.06%, as first reported by Gulf Daily News (Bahrain) and picked up by CX-industry aggregator Renascence. Saudi AI tool adoption more than doubles to 45.2% of internet users

AI & techSaudi ArabiaMarket data10 Aug 2026 · recent

The Communications, Space and Technology Commission's (CST) Saudi Internet Report for 2025 shows AI-tool adoption among Saudi internet users more than doubled year-on-year to 45.2%. Usage was highest among 20–29 year-olds (55.7%) and 10–19 year-olds (53.4%), falling to 14.8% among 60–74 year-olds; usage among females (52.8%) outpaced males (39.4%). Information searches were the most common use case (80.8%), followed by idea generation (40.1%) and study/education (37.7%), with 17.3% applying AI to work tasks. A separate GASTAT report cited in the same coverage put business AI adoption at 33.1% in 2025 (+20% year-on-year), led by information and communications (61.1%), financial and insurance activities (52.9%) and education (51%), relevant baseline context for any CX/retail operator gauging how AI-literate the Saudi consumer and workforce base has become.
