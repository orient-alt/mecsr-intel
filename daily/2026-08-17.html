Market Knight Frank: Saudi retail spending and mall occupancy climb in H1 2026

DestinationsSaudi ArabiaMarket data5 Aug 2026 · recent

Knight Frank's Summer 2026 Saudi Arabia retail market report found consumer spending rose 6.8% year-on-year to SAR 425 billion in Q1 2026, with e-commerce spending up 42% to SAR 98.4 billion. Mall occupancy held stable at 91% in Riyadh (average lease rates up 1.2% YoY to SAR 2,650/sqm), improved to 88% in Jeddah, and reached 94% in Dammam. Riyadh's "super-regional" lifestyle retail stock stands at roughly 485,000 sqm across 28 schemes at 96% occupancy; Jeddah's lifestyle retail stock is around 291,000 sqm across 19 schemes, with a further 277,600 sqm due by 2029. This is a distinct Knight Frank release from the Riyadh GLA-growth forecast already reported on 3 August 2026, the two cover different data sets (occupancy/spending vs. pipeline GLA).

Status Update Morocco Mall Marrakech opening pushed back again: now spring 2027

DestinationsMoroccoStatus update22 Apr 2026 · background

Aksal Group, led by Salwa (Saloua) Idrissi Akhannouch, has postponed the opening of its Marrakech shopping complex, a 130,000+ sqm scheme on the Ourika road, over 1 billion dirhams in investment, from the previously targeted summer 2026 to spring 2027. The delay is described as a deliberate decision rather than a technical one: construction (shell and core, landscaping, finishing touches) is largely complete, but the developer wants to avoid opening with high vacancy amid softer Moroccan consumer spending, tied in part to regional conflict effects on purchasing power and the recent closure/departure of several apparel brands in Morocco (Jennyfer, Terranova, Tape-à-l'œil). This resolves a "summer 2026, unconfirmed" watch-list item first flagged in the 10 and 12 August reports.

Member News Financial Lulu Retail profit falls as shoppers cut non-food spending

MembersUAEFinancial14 Aug 2026 · in windowMECSR member

Member Lulu Retail Holdings filed Q2 2026 results with the Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange on 13 August, covered by regional media the next day. Revenue fell 2.3% year-on-year to $2.0 billion as consumer spending weakened, particularly in non-food and electronics categories. Saudi Arabia revenue dropped 11% to $338 million; UAE revenue was nearly flat at $756 million. Net profit fell more than a quarter in the quarter (six-month bottom line down 30%). E-commerce revenue grew 53% year-on-year. Despite a 20% decline in cash, the board approved an $84 million H1 dividend. CEO Saifee Rupawala said the company "returned to modest growth in July." This result arrives roughly a week earlier than the "H1 results due 19 August" date carried on this report's watch list since 11 August, the earlier date should be treated as superseded.

MembersEgyptDeal / JV18 May 2026 · backgroundMECSR member

KONE inaugurates enhanced Customer Service Center in Jeddah

CXSaudi ArabiaCX2 Aug 2026 · recent

KONE marked the official inauguration of its enhanced Customer Service Center in Jeddah, reinforcing its regional commitment to customer support in Saudi Arabia. The center provides round-the-clock phone support for service requests, maintenance inquiries and equipment-related assistance, staffed by locally based bilingual (Arabic/English) care professionals who handled approximately 11,000 customer interactions in 2025. KONE has set a target of answering customer calls within 20 seconds, part of a broader push to make expert elevator/escalator support more accessible to residential, commercial and landmark-development customers across the Kingdom.

UAE launches strategic government workstream of its national Agentic AI Project

AI & techUAEAI / tech10 Aug 2026 · recent

More than 100 federal officials attended a workshop launching the strategic government track of the UAE's national Agentic AI Project, organised by the National Committee for the Agentic AI Project. The initiative aims to convert 50% of government operations, services and tasks into agentic-AI-driven models within two years, under the UAE Government 4.0 framework, and covers seven pillars including strategy, foresight, policy, governance and government performance. It builds on the UAE's first cohort of specialised AI agents (procurement, tax auditing, customer happiness and technical support) launched in May 2026 and the HR AI Agent already serving 50,000+ federal employees, this run's news is the formal kickoff of the follow-on strategic/government-services workstream rather than a repeat of those earlier launches.
