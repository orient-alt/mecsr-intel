Method

How this intelligence is researched

One research run every weekday at 08:00 Dubai. Friday's run adds the weekly summary and a verification batch of directory listings.

What each run covers

  • 🏬 Shopping destinations & pipeline: new malls, mixed-use schemes, outlet centres and retail parks, expansions, openings, closures and supply data across the 24+ markets of the MECSR Directory.
  • 🏢 Member company news: projects, results, leadership, deals and awards for MECSR member organisations.
  • 📞 Customer experience & service: GCC customer-experience programmes, awards, contact-centre and service news.
  • 🤖 AI & retail technology: AI agents, voice and chat, personalisation and retail-tech launches in the region, plus a few global items that matter here.
  • 📚 Reports, PDFs & manuals: market reports, annual reports and investor presentations, exchange filings, tenant fit-out manuals, design and regulatory guidelines, CX standards.

Sources

  • Tier A: the operator or developer itself, exchange filings and annual reports, government and regulators, JLL / CBRE / Knight Frank / Cushman & Wakefield / Colliers / Savills, named trade press (Retail ME, Arabian Business, Gulf Business, Zawya).
  • Tier B: established regional and international media with named editorial teams; needs a second source for figures.
  • Tier C: discovery only (portals, social posts, aggregators): used to find a story, never cited for it.
  • Tier D: never used: lead-database and scraper sites, unattributed or machine-generated pages, and MECSR's own directory (no circular citation).

Dates

Every item is dated from the article itself: the page's published-time metadata, its byline or a dated URL, never from the search snippet. Items are labelled in window (published in the run's coverage window), recent (up to 90 days) or background (older). Monday covers Friday to Sunday; Tuesday to Friday cover the previous day; a missed day is picked up by the next run.

Quiet days

A day with fewer than three verified items still gets a page, marked as a quiet day, with whatever cleared the bar and the watch list. Nothing unverified is added to make a page look fuller.

What is not on this site

No personal contact data, no e-mail addresses or phone numbers, and no membership-prospect or CRM information. Those stay in MECSR's internal files.
