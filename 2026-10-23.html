Cenomi Centers wins dismissal of the Mall of Dhahran owner's claims, subject to appeal

MembersSaudi Arabia · DhahranFinancial22 Oct 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: Medium→ 2027 Directory: Check first

Cenomi Centers told Tadawul that a ruling issued on 21 Oct 2026 dismissed the lawsuit brought by the owner of Mall of Dhahran, which it was notified of in March. The owner had sought SAR 161.853 million in fair rental value for mall and expansion areas not covered by a contract, SAR 103.397 million in rent for the part of the mall damaged by fire and SAR 523.028 million in revenues from the property; ANB Capital put the total at SAR 788.3 million in June.

The owner may appeal within 30 days of the date set for receiving the judgment, after which it becomes final, and no financial impact was stated. ANB Capital also reported that Cenomi completed a phased handover of the mall in April 2026, when its lease term expired.

Why it matters: If the ruling stands it removes SAR 788.3 million of claims against a member, and the handover leaves one of the Eastern Province's best-known malls outside Cenomi's portfolio and without a directory entry.

1

UAE cuts the tourist VAT refund wait from about nine days to one business day with an AI platform

CXUAE · Abu DhabiLaunch21 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: High

The Federal Tax Authority and Planet, the authorised operator of the UAE's VAT refund scheme for tourists, have launched an AI-powered digital platform that cuts the total wait for a refund to one business day, from about nine days. WAM says verification uses AI-powered facial recognition, with data entry through Planet's mobile app, in a fully digital and paperless process at Zayed International, Dubai International and Sharjah International airports. No platform name, start date or retailer figures were published.

Why it matters: The refund is the last step of a tourist's mall shopping journey, so a one-day refund matters to retailers ahead of Dubai Shopping Festival (4 Dec 2026 to 17 Jan 2027) and is a ready case study in AI-enabled service for MECSC.

AI & retail technology

1

Walmart opens its systems to third-party AI shopping agents, with limits set by the shopper

AI & techGlobalAI / tech22 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Walmart announced on 21 Oct a Walmart Agent Gateway that lets AI assistants such as OpenAI's ChatGPT, Google's Gemini and Meta's Muse connect directly to Walmart and Sam's Club systems to search for products and complete purchases. Agent Profiles, rolling out next month, let shoppers authorise individual agents, see each agent's purchases separately, set a spending limit or approve every transaction, and revoke access at any time.

Agents cannot access payment card data and can be blocked for breaking company rules, and the tools are open to all account holders without a Walmart+ membership.

Why it matters: Walmart's opt-in rules for agent identity, permissions and spending limits are the counter-model to the Amazon and eBay blocks reported on 19 Oct, and a template GCC retailers and mall apps can benchmark before agent traffic reaches the region.

The Star (Reuters) · 22 Oct 2026 ↗BMediaPost · 22 Oct 2026 ↗B
