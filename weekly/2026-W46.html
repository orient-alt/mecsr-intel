The week in brief

  • Week 46 (9 to 13 November): 40 findings and 8 library documents across five editions, with Friday's checks of directory listings.
  • Saudi Arabia: Cenomi Centers' Q3 profit fell 19% to SAR 404.9m against a year-earlier insurance gain as revenue rose 10% to SAR 606.2m, and its chief executive kept Westfield Riyadh on target to start operating in Q4 2026; Knowledge Economic City's Q3 profit rose to SAR 99.7m; Makkah Construction took a 25-year lease on Al Misfalah Park for a commercial and leisure destination; Al-Andalus Mall's expansion in Jeddah is fully leased; and the FII Institute set 25 to 28 October 2027 for the postponed FII10.
  • Kuwait and Riyadh: Mabanee says Aventura Mall is built and will open in November, and that it is preparing to open The Avenues Riyadh on 27 March 2027.
  • UAE: CBRE put Q3 mall occupancy at 97% in Dubai and 96% in Abu Dhabi, with about 240,000 sqm of retail GLA due in 2027, while JLL reported Dubai's new retail leases down 7.3% year on year; Lulu Retail's Q3 revenue rose 2% to $1.9bn as online sales jumped 53%; Nakheel unveiled Palm Springs next to Palm Jebel Ali; the Dubai Shopping Festival announced its opening programme; and DEAL Holdings' FAB Group plans at least 25 UAE food and beverage sites.
  • Loyalty and service: Majid Al Futtaim's SHARE added conversions with Emirates Skywards and travel bookings with Expedia; Landmark Group alerted some Shukran members to unauthorised access to personal data; Amazon.ae became the first online retailer on the UAE's Essential Goods Prices Platform; Egypt's central bank set rules for banks' agents; and Saudi health bodies won 36 awards at the International Customer Experience Awards.
  • AI and retail technology: OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT with product feeds and Business Agents; Zalando launched an agent that buys from other retailers inside its app; CrediMax and Mastercard will explore AI shopping agents for Bahrain's merchants; etisalat by e& started selling Meta's AI glasses; and Creators HQ and YouTube will train more than 1,000 creators to sell through YouTube Shopping.
  • Tenants and formats: JYSK opened its 18th Türkiye store at Mall of İstanbul, U.S. Polo Assn. opened a concept store at Koru Florya, Elya Center AVM in Ankara started leasing, the world's first KFC Muse opened at JBR in Dubai, and Korea Town by Boutiqaat opened two stores in Qatar.
  • Directory checks: Friday's checks found a placeholder card for Saudi Downtown Company's 12 projects, two cards for Golden Gate in New Cairo, a passed 2024 opening for Madinah Gate, a larger leasing area for The Point in Abha, and Al Shubaily Grand Mall listed under another spelling.
