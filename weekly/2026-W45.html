The week in brief

  • Week 45 (2 to 6 November): 36 findings and 9 library documents across five editions, and the fourth Friday check of directory listings (16 listings).
  • Saudi pipeline: PIF's Saudi Downtown Company launched Al Khobar Downtown, an 829,000 sqm waterfront scheme with more than 139,000 sqm of retail and dining around the mall Cenomi is developing; Cenomi signed a SAR 825m design-and-build contract for the Madinah Downtown mall and offices; and Riyadh Municipality opened bidding on its first joint land investment, a 30,000 sqm mixed-use plot.
  • Results: Emaar's mall and retail leasing revenue rose 10% to Dh5.2bn in nine months at about 98% occupancy while hotel occupancy fell to 63%; Seef Properties' nine-month profit fell 30% on weaker leisure and spending; Jabal Omar swung to a Q3 loss as land-sale gains dropped out; Emaar The Economic City's loss widened; and Cenomi Centers had still not filed by 6 Nov.
  • Tenants and new schemes: M&S will open its largest Foodhall outside the UK at Dubai Festival City in December; OVS took direct control of its UAE stores; Sports Direct entered Africa at two Nairobi malls; Waitrose is the first tenant of DMCC's Uptown Place; Fashion House Group will develop an outlet at Grand Uptown Mall in New October City; Edamah and Kanoo will develop Pier 1890 in Manama; and Bishkek's Ala-Too market opened for the Madina bazaar's traders.
  • Members and loyalty: Magrabi agreed to buy Dubai Optical and its 15 Dubai Optical-branded UAE stores, and Majid Al Futtaim brought its SHARE rewards programme to Carrefour Kenya and its 1.4 million MyClub members.
  • Spending and service: Turkish mall sales per sqm rose 29.7% in September, in line with inflation, led by entertainment; UAE gold jewellery demand fell 29% in Q3; the UAE's Global Star Rating covered 179 government service channels; and Dubai launched an Access Club for repeat visitors and will pilot nol-Jaywan payment cards.
  • AI and retail technology: DoorDash agreed to buy Instacart for about $17bn, and Instacart is winding down its Caper smart carts; Amazon opened its Alexa for Shopping agent to outside merchants; Salesforce agreed to buy voice AI firm Cresta; Abu Dhabi signed agreements with OpenAI; and holiday surveys show shoppers want to approve any purchase an AI makes for them.
  • 2027 Directory: sheet 10 gained the Madinah Downtown and Al Khobar Downtown malls (Add to pipeline), Pier 1890, Uptown Place and the Grand Uptown Mall outlet (Watch), and the Ala-Too market, Sarit Centre, Galleria Mall and Astana's Jurt (Check first); Friday's check changed 7 of 16 listings, including two cards for one Pixel Plaza, Silicon Central trading since 2021 and 'Masat Mall' being Hamat's Masar Mall, while 8 could not be confirmed from owners' sources.
