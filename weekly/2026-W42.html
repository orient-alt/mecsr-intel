The week in brief

  • First week of the combined MECSR and MECSC edition: 62 findings and 32 library documents across the editions of 13 to 16 October, including a catch-up of 26 September to 13 October.
  • Dubai led the pipeline news: Dubai Retail's Skylight Mall (1.3m sq ft GLA, 2027) and 38 new outlets at Ibn Battuta Mall, Majid Al Futtaim's The Hangout at Mall of the Emirates, the former Lamcy Plaza returning as The Plaza in H1 2027, and a 'Dubai Mall Plaza and Hotel' teaser from Emaar's founder.
  • Members made big moves: Al Othaim launched Al Othaim Developments with a SAR16bn+ plan, and Aldar followed its AED15bn partnership with Arada by buying AED1.1bn of Aljada homes, its first Sharjah investment, while planning a fourth Dubai project with Dubai Holding.
  • Agentic AI moved from pilots to rules: every UAE federal AI agent must connect to a 'control tower', the UAE and the World Economic Forum launched a $1 million Agentic AI Award, and Majid Al Futtaim says AI agents already play a role in 9% of its customer transactions.
  • On the shop floor, Max Fashion brought AI virtual try-on to City Center Doha, Lulu put Odigo robots in UAE stores as a retail media channel, and OpenAI added try-on to ChatGPT shopping.
  • Türkiye stayed busy: Florentia Village Istanbul and Merkez Ankara AVM are trading, N City in Diyarbakır is being repositioned as an outlet mall, and flooding closed Vadistanbul's car parks on 15 October.
  • 2027 Directory: the first Friday check of 15 listings against owners' own disclosures found 9 changes, including Aventura Mall now due at the end of October, The Avenues Riyadh at about 361,202 sqm GLA for March 2027, and Westfield Dammam at over 58,000 sqm.
  • Next: Primark at Doha Festival City (29 Oct), Aventura Mall (end Oct), Q3 results from late October, the UAE services announcement in November, Saudi flight disruption after the Riyadh airport closure, and GITEX (7 to 11 Dec) against RCM Jeddah (7 to 8 Dec).
