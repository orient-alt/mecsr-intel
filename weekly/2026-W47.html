The week in brief

  • Week 47 (16 to 20 November): 46 findings and 12 library documents across five editions, with Friday's checks of directory listings.
  • Kuwait: Mabanee's Aventura Mall opened to visitors on 15 Nov with 15 Alshaya stores, among them H&M, Boots and Shake Shack, and Mabanee confirmed that The Avenues Riyadh opens on 27 March 2027.
  • Saudi Arabia: the Cabinet approved a unified Consumer Protection Law with fines of up to SAR 8 million; Knowledge Economic City's shareholders approved renaming it Al-Jiwar Economic City, and it expects two Madinah shopping centres to start trading in 2027; Al Othaim Markets lost SAR 39.26m in Q3 after problems with an ERP rollout.
  • UAE: Majid Al Futtaim will build an AED 260m staff parking facility to free more than 1,000 shopper spaces at Mall of the Emirates by December 2027; Alef Group's 06 Mall will carry a façade screen from CITIX MENA; Spinneys expects its first stores in Kuwait and the Philippines in Q1 2027; and talabat raised its 2026 guidance again.
  • Egypt, Türkiye, Iraq and Kazakhstan: Lulu opened a hypermarket in Obour City; Civilia launched STEEP Mall in Sheikh Zayed City; ZEISS opened at İstanbul AVM and KİP opened its first Iraq store at Grand Majidi Mall in Erbil; CarrefourSA opened its 56th Gurme store; Kanyon revived Radyo Kanyon; and Dunkin' opened its first Kazakhstan restaurant in Almaty.
  • Service and AI: Dubai ranked first in the UN's Local Online Service Index 2026 and approved a City AI Agent, and Dubai Municipality unveiled D.Mind; the EU's proposed Digital Fairness Act would give consumers a right to human customer service; and Visa opened Agentic Ready to merchants after OpenAI's ChatGPT Wallet let its agents pay for purchases.
  • Directory checks: Souq Sharq in Kuwait City was emptied for a refurbishment PPP won by United Real Estate Company, The Avenues - Bahrain's western expansion opened in Q2 2025, Majid Al Futtaim gives City Centre Bahrain 153k m2 and Mall of Egypt 170k m2 of GLA, and the directory's 'D2 Community Centre' is DAMAC Hills 2 Community Centre.
