One Retail signs a master franchise to bring Spanish beauty chain Primor to Morocco from 2027

DestinationsMoroccoMarket entry21 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

One Retail, the retail platform of Morocco's H&S Group, has signed an exclusive master franchise agreement with Spanish beauty retailer Primor, Morocco World News reported on 21 Oct 2026. It plans to build a Primor network gradually across Morocco's main cities, with the first openings planned for 2027. No cities, malls or store numbers were given.

Why it matters: A new international beauty brand for Moroccan malls to court as a tenant from 2027.

Primark opens its first Qatar store at Doha Festival City, eight days ahead of the announced date

DestinationsQatar · DohaMarket entry21 Oct 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Update listing · Medium

Alshaya Group opened Primark's first store in Qatar at Doha Festival City on 21 Oct 2026, eight days before the 29 Oct date it announced on 2 Sep. The store has over 52,000 sq ft of shopping space on three floors and over 400 staff, and Alshaya calls Qatar Primark's 21st international market and its fourth country in the Middle East.

A second Qatar store will follow at Villaggio Mall and a second Kuwait store at Aventura Mall early next year, and Primark's first Saudi store is due in March 2027.

Why it matters: An international value-fashion anchor has entered Qatar through Doha Festival City and has named its next sites at Villaggio Mall and Mabanee's Aventura Mall, which touches three directory listings.

Awqaf Dubai launches an AED73m two-storey endowment shopping centre with 40 outlets in Hind City

DestinationsUAE · DubaiNew property20 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Check first

Awqaf Dubai, the Endowment and Minors' Trust Foundation, has launched an AED73 million endowment shopping centre in Hind City, Dubai, with Voco Investments, the Dubai Media Office reported on 20 Oct 2026. The two-storey centre will have 40 retail outlets on a 90,400 sq ft plot; the AED73 million is made up of AED50 million of construction costs and land valued at AED23 million.

Annual rental income is put at about AED8.4 million and total returns to Awqaf Dubai at an estimated AED68 million over the investment period. Completion is scheduled within two years; no name, GLA or tenants were given.

Why it matters: It adds a new community centre to the Dubai pipeline and shows an endowment (waqf) model for funding retail property.

City Centre Muscat marks 25 years and Mall of Oman five, with 180 million and 53 million visitors to date

MembersOman · MuscatEvent21 Oct 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Update listing · Low

Majid Al Futtaim marked 25 years of City Centre Muscat, its first business in Oman, which opened in October 2001 and has had more than 180 million visitors, and five years of Mall of Oman, which opened in September 2021 with LEED Platinum certification and has had 53 million visitors.

MAF says City Centre Muscat nearly doubled to approximately 60,500 square metres in its 2007 expansion and gained 10,000 square metres and 60 stores in an OMR 35 million redevelopment completed in 2015. Snow Oman at Mall of Oman, opened on 24 Dec 2022, covers 14,830 square metres, and the Oman portfolio draws more than 25 million visitors a year.

Why it matters: MAF's own opening dates and space figures confirm two directory listings, and the anniversaries are a ready hook for MECSR content and awards.

Majid Al Futtaim · 21 Oct 2026 ↗A

Aldar and ADIB launch Darna co-branded cards that earn up to 10% back at Aldar malls

MembersUAE · Abu DhabiLaunch21 Oct 2026 · in windowMECSR memberConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Article idea

Aldar and Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank have launched the ADIB Aldar Visa Infinite and Visa Platinum Covered Cards, linked to Darna, Aldar's loyalty programme in the Live Aldar app. Cardholders earn up to 10% value back in Darna Points at participating Aldar malls, hotels, golf courses, restaurants and entertainment venues, up to 1.5% on eligible Aldar property and Aldar Education spending and up to 2% on eligible international spending.

Benefits include a welcome reward of up to 20,000 Darna Points (equivalent to AED 1,000), complimentary Darna Gold or Platinum status and up to four complimentary valet parking sessions a month at selected UAE locations.

Why it matters: A mall landlord putting its own loyalty currency on a bank card is a retention and data model that other members and MECSC's customer-experience programmes can benchmark.

Zawya (Aldar release) · 21 Oct 2026 ↗A

Salesforce agrees to buy customer engagement platform Braze for about $5.66bn

AI & techGlobalDeal / JV20 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Salesforce has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Braze for $47.50 a share in cash, about $5.66 billion in enterprise value, it announced on 20 Oct 2026. Braze runs real-time customer messaging across apps, websites, email, SMS and WhatsApp, with AI choosing content, offers, channels and timing, and has about 2,770 customers.

Salesforce will use it to strengthen Marketing Cloud's cross-channel personalisation alongside Data 360, Customer 360 and Agentforce. Closing is expected in Salesforce's fiscal year 2028, subject to Braze shareholder and regulatory approvals.

Why it matters: It folds a large customer-messaging platform, WhatsApp included, into Salesforce's agentic CRM suite, which matters to Gulf retailers and mall operators choosing loyalty and marketing tools.

Salesforce · 20 Oct 2026 ↗A
