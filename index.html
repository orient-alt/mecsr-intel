Dubai's Services 360 policy: 1,474 government services redesigned, customer happiness at 94%

CXUAE · DubaiPolicy7 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

At the Government Best Practices Series 2026 on 7 October, the General Secretariat of The Executive Council of Dubai reported three-year results for its Services 360 policy across 1,474 services and 28 entities: required documents down 61%, visits down 85%, waiting time down 96% and completion time down 51%, with customer happiness at 94% and trust at 90%. GDRFA Dubai cut waiting time from 11.7 to 1.7 minutes and lifted happiness from 92% to 99%.

Why it matters: Published, measurable service-redesign results that mall and retail CX teams can benchmark against, and case-study material for RCM Jeddah CX sessions and the MENA Awards.

Meta and Sierra propose the Personal Agent Protocol, an open standard for consumers' AI agents dealing with businesses

AI & techGlobalLaunch6 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Sierra and Meta announced the Personal Agent Protocol on 6 October 2026: an open, OAuth-based standard for how a consumer's AI agent signs in, identifies itself and completes tasks with a business through its website, its APIs or the business's own agent. Consumers choose read-only or write access and businesses set limits. Named partners include Genesys, NiCE, Shopify, Stripe and Walmart; a v0.1 specification is due later in October.

Why it matters: Contact-centre and commerce platforms many GCC retailers run on are writing the rules for agent-led shopping and service requests.
