Makkah Construction takes a 25-year lease on Al Misfalah Park to turn it into a commercial and leisure destination

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · MakkahPipeline11 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

Makkah Construction and Development Co. said on 11 Nov that it had signed a 25-year lease on 10 Nov with Al Balad Al Ameen for Development and Urban Regeneration Co. to invest in, develop and operate Al Misfalah Park in Makkah, a site of approximately 109,000 sq m. Annual rent is approximately SAR 25.8m excluding VAT, rising 10% every five contractual years, with a 30-month grace period spread over the first five years; the term runs from the date the site is handed over. The company plans an integrated destination combining green space with commercial and recreational activities and expects recurring rental income once it is built and trading. No name, retail area or timeline has been given.

Why it matters: Adds a commercial and leisure destination on a large central Makkah site to the Saudi pipeline, with the land cost published by a listed developer.

Qatar's National Product Week opens in hypermarkets and malls, with displays at LuLu, Monoprix at Place Vendôme and Al Meera

DestinationsQatar · DohaEvent5 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

Qatar's Ministry of Commerce and Industry has launched National Product Week, QNA reported on 5 Nov, opening displays of Qatari products at LuLu Hypermarket in Al Gharafa, Monoprix at Place Vendôme and Al Meera in Ain Khaled, with Carrefour at City Center Mall and Rawabi in Al Gharafa due to follow that Friday. The programme also includes workshops with the Ministry of Finance and Qatar Development Bank and school visits to national factories. QNA reports that some participating outlets recorded increases of up to 75 percent in sales of national products during 2025.

Why it matters: Government-backed local-product campaigns give Qatar's hypermarket anchors and malls a recurring promotion slot.

Amazon.ae becomes the first online retailer on the UAE's Essential Goods Prices Platform

CXUAECX11 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

The UAE Ministry of Economy and Tourism has signed a memorandum of understanding with Amazon UAE that adds Amazon.ae's prices to its Essential Goods Prices Platform, making it the first e-commerce retailer on the government price-comparison service; the Ministry said on Wednesday 11 Nov that the price records are already live. The platform, launched in April 2026, lets shoppers compare the prices of everyday goods across major retailers in basket comparisons. Undersecretary Abdulla Ahmed Al Saleh and Ronaldo Mouchawar, Vice President of Amazon Middle East, Africa and Turkey, signed in the presence of Minister Abdulla bin Touq Al Marri. Gulf News notes that the announcement does not say whether delivery charges, online promotions or membership discounts are counted in the comparisons.

Why it matters: Online and in-store grocery prices now sit side by side on a government service, adding price-transparency pressure on the supermarket and hypermarket anchors of UAE malls.

The world's first KFC Muse opens at JBR in Dubai, a personalised dine-in format KFC plans to carry into Saudi Arabia and Qatar

CXUAE · DubaiLaunch10 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Retail ME reports the world's first KFC Muse opening at Jumeirah Beach Residence (JBR) in Dubai, the home market of KFC's franchise partner Americana Restaurants. The format moves KFC from quick service towards a fuller dining experience built on personalisation and hospitality, including KFC MIX'D, where customers choose their chicken, sauces and seasonings with staff guidance. A second Muse is planned for Seoul, and KFC plans to bring elements of Muse into new and remodelled restaurants in markets such as Saudi Arabia and Qatar. The article, an interview with Benjamin Dubost, General Manager of KFC Middle East, North Africa and Central Asia, gives no opening date, size or venue within JBR.

Why it matters: A global fast-food brand rebuilding service around staff-guided personalisation, with plans for Saudi and Qatari restaurants, is a reference point for F&B at malls and leisure destinations.

OpenAI lets businesses claim ChatGPT profiles with product feeds and adds Business Agents, first for US online sellers

AI & techGlobalLaunch10 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta on 10 Nov. Through Business Hub, a company can claim and verify its profile, update key details, add product feeds with pricing and availability, and see aggregated insights such as mentions, views and website clicks. Business Agents let people chat with a company's agent about its products or services, rolling out first to eligible e-commerce and online service businesses in the US; businesses can sponsor their agent as the destination for labelled ChatGPT Ads, and Shopify merchants can connect through Business Hub or the Shopify admin. OpenAI says it does not share users' conversations or Business Agent chats with businesses.

Why it matters: AI assistants are turning into a storefront and service channel, so Gulf retailers and mall brands will need to claim and manage how they appear in them.
