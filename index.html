Mega Alma-Ata puts up an interactive New Year toy-factory installation under its dome

DestinationsKazakhstan · AlmatyEvent20 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Kursiv reported on 20 Nov that the Mega Alma-Ata shopping and entertainment centre in Almaty has installed a New Year attraction built as a working toy factory, with moving parts, festive music and buttons that let visitors start processes inside it. Elves in traditional Kazakh costume sort letters and pack gifts, and a reindeer sleigh appears to float under the centre's dome. Most social-media reactions were positive, though some users questioned decorating before the 16 December Independence Day holiday; the article names no organiser, run dates or cost.

Why it matters: An early, locally themed interactive installation shows Almaty malls competing on experience for New Year footfall.

Hassan Allam Holding to develop NEVERA, a 36 million sqm tourism and commercial destination in west Cairo with retail for international brands

DestinationsEgypt · Giza (west Greater Cairo)Pipeline18 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Nevera Egypt for Sustainable Development signed a strategic partnership agreement with Hassan Allam Holding on 18 Nov, in the presence of Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly, to develop NEVERA, a 36 million sqm tourism, hospitality, entertainment and commercial destination along the Middle Ring Road in Giza, near Sphinx International Airport, the Giza Pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum. Hassan Allam Holding is master developer and will carry out the real estate and hospitality development through its subsidiary Grova Developments. Plans include about 6,000 hotel rooms from three to seven stars (nearly 40% of the rooms targeted around the Pyramids and the museum), shopping districts aimed at international brands, a theme park, a convention centre with exhibition grounds and a high-speed rail station; the project will be built in phases, and no investment value, retail area or dates have been published.

Why it matters: A tourism-led destination beside the Grand Egyptian Museum adds international-brand retail to west Cairo's pipeline, though its retail size and timing are not yet known.

Jordan's trade ministry widens market and online checks for White Friday sales and requires prices before and after discount to be shown

CXJordan · AmmanPolicy11 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Jordan's Ministry of Industry, Trade and Supply said on 11 Nov that it is expanding field inspections across the country's markets and monitoring online offers during the White Friday discount season, with a focus on misleading promotions on social media. Traders must clearly show prices before and after the discount with full details, discounts must have documented legal grounds and deceptive tactics are banned. Shoppers can report complaints, violations and misleading advertisements through the ministry's complaint channels; the statement gives no penalties or season dates.

Why it matters: Retailers and mall tenants in Jordan running White Friday offers face closer checks on price display and social-media promotions, a recurring customer-trust issue for MECSC's service-quality work.

Vusion offers about SEK 980.4m in cash for electronic shelf label maker Pricer, with Pricer's board recommending the offer

AI & techGlobalDeal / JV17 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

VusionGroup announced on 17 Nov a recommended cash offer of SEK 6.00 a share for Sweden's Pricer, valuing all its shares at approximately SEK 980.4 million and about 29.6% above the class B share's close on 16 Nov. Pricer's board unanimously recommends acceptance and its largest shareholder, with about 14.15% of the shares, has irrevocably undertaken to accept; the acceptance period is expected to run from about 8 Dec 2026 to 5 Jan 2027, and completion requires Vusion to own more than 90% of the shares, a condition it may waive. Vusion says more than 780 million of its electronic shelf labels are deployed in 80,000 stores, and Pricer says it has delivered over 380 million to more than 28,000 stores in more than 80 countries.

Why it matters: Consolidation among electronic shelf label suppliers affects the technology and pricing choices of Gulf grocers and hypermarkets planning digital shelves and in-store retail media.

REWE ends its Pick & Go checkout-free store test in Germany

AI & techGlobalStatus update17 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

REWE Group is ending the operational test of Pick & Go, a camera-based format that lets shoppers skip scanning and pay at a terminal by card or cash, in its supermarkets in Germany, the latest of which opened in Hannover in spring 2026, Lebensmittel Praxis reported on 17 Nov. Christoph Eltze, REWE Group's Chief Digital and Technology Officer, said the test's learning goals had been met and the findings will now be evaluated, and that REWE will keep testing computer vision and other automated checkout concepts. Retail Technology Innovation Hub says the project ran with technology provider Trigo from 2021.

Why it matters: A large European grocer stepping back from checkout-free stores is a useful signal on the format's economics for Gulf grocers and convenience chains weighing similar technology.

Lebensmittel Praxis · 17 Nov 2026 ↗BRetail Technology Innovation Hub · 17 Nov 2026 ↗B

Amazon.ae brings its Alexa for Shopping AI assistant, in beta, into its 12-day White Friday sale in the UAE

AI & techUAELaunch19 Nov 2026 · recentConfidence: High

Amazon UAE's White Friday sale runs from 19 to 30 Nov across more than 30 product categories, and Amazon is integrating Alexa for Shopping, its AI shopping assistant recently rolled out in beta for UAE customers, Gulf Business reported on 19 Nov. Free for signed-in Amazon.ae customers in the Amazon Shopping app and on the website, the assistant summarises product information and reviews, compares products, shows up to a year of price history, gives personalised recommendations and helps build baskets through conversational requests; the report does not say it can complete purchases. A HarrisX survey commissioned by Amazon says 85% of UAE shoppers are more likely to buy during sales this season, 75% spend more time reading reviews or checking product details and 71% plan purchases in advance.

Why it matters: A conversational shopping assistant is now in front of UAE online shoppers in the peak sales fortnight before the Dubai Shopping Festival, raising expectations of retailer and mall apps on product comparison and price transparency.

Gulf Business · 19 Nov 2026 ↗A
