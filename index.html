Astana's Asia Park makes 1.4 billion tenge net profit in nine months on 2.6 billion tenge of rental income

DestinationsKazakhstan · AstanaFinancial17 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

TOO Asia Park, which owns the Asia Park shopping and entertainment centre in Astana, made a net profit of 1.4 billion tenge in January to September 2026 on revenue of 2.6 billion tenge, all of it rental income, Kursiv reported on 17 Nov from the company's financial statements. Its issuer page on the Kazakhstan Stock Exchange, where it has three privately placed bond issues, shows net profit of 1,358,638 thousand tenge, operating income of 2,591,230 thousand tenge and total assets of 11,654,465 thousand tenge in the column dated 1 Oct 2026. Kursiv says Arcada Group (95%) and Arcada (5%), both of Karaganda and owned by entrepreneur Alexey Nefedov, hold the company, and that a reconstruction adding a business centre and offices is under way.

Why it matters: Exchange-filed results for a Central Asian mall are rare: they confirm the owner of a listed directory property and show its rent-driven earnings ahead of a reconstruction that adds offices.

Türkiye's shopping centre association AYD holds its 17th Shopping Economy Summit in Istanbul on 8 Dec

DestinationsTürkiye · IstanbulEvent17 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: High

AYD, Türkiye's association of shopping centres and investors, will hold its 17th Shopping Economy Summit on Tuesday 8 Dec 2026 at Swissôtel The Bosphorus in Istanbul, organised with Alkaş and with Fiba Commercial Properties as main sponsor (Mall Report and AVM Dergi, 17 Nov). The programme covers economic resilience, global investment opportunities with Aamal Company chief executive Rashid Bin Ali Al Mansoori, the carbon economy after COP31, luxury retail with ECE Türkiye and Beymen Group, and governance in family companies, and Deputy Trade Minister Mahmut Gürcan is among the opening speakers. ZEISS opens its first Türkiye Vision Center at İstanbul AVM, the new shopping centre in the İstanbul Finance Centre

BrandsTürkiye · IstanbulMarket entry17 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

ZEISS has opened Türkiye's first ZEISS Vision Center, an optical store built around ZEISS lenses and its VISUFIT 1000 fitting system, at İstanbul Alışveriş Merkezi (İstanbul AVM) in the İstanbul Finance Centre, Mall Report reported on 17 Nov in an item quoting Mustafa Kemal Köksal, chief executive of Beta Optik, ZEISS Vision Care's distributor in Türkiye. Fashion start-up Daydream launches an AI stylist agent that can complete purchases with one-click checkout

AI & techGlobalLaunch17 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Daydream, the fashion search start-up founded by Julie Bornstein, launched Daydream Agent on 17 Nov, TechCrunch reported. The agent remembers each shopper's style, fit and budget, tries items on virtually from an uploaded photo, plans outfits with a connected wardrobe and calendar, and tracks sales and brand drops with weekly updates; saved items go on a persistent 'clothing rack', and the agent can complete a purchase through one-click checkout. The report does not say how each purchase is approved or in which countries the agent is available.

Why it matters: Another agent that shops across many brands' catalogues and checks out for the buyer, adding to the open question of how GCC fashion retailers and malls open product data and checkout to outside AI agents.

TechCrunch · 17 Nov 2026 ↗B

Sharjah's 06 Mall will carry a façade screen for 3D advertising, with smartboards across Al Mamsha, from CITIX MENA

AI & techUAE · SharjahDeal / JV17 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

CITIX MENA will install 'Jumbo', a large-format digital screen for premium and naked-eye 3D campaigns, on the façade of Alef Group's 06 Mall in Sharjah, Campaign Middle East reported on 17 Nov. The rollout also adds roadside and pedestrian smartboards across Alef's Al Mamsha community, built on CITIX MENA's Citix Panel to show real-time information, widgets and brand messages that adapt to conditions. CITIX MENA, an urban technology company working in Sharjah, Dubai and Abu Dhabi, is offering the formats to brands, media agencies and retail partners; the report gives no screen size or go-live date.

Why it matters: A community mall turning its façade and neighbourhood into sellable digital media is a GCC example of retail media income beyond rent.
