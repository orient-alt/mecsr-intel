Al Shubaily Grand Mall in Al Khobar begins a phased soft opening, with the grand opening planned for 2027

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · Al KhobarOpening29 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium→ 2027 Directory: Update listing · High

Arabian AlEsaar says Al Shubaily Grand Mall in Al Khobar began a phased soft opening on 29 Oct 2026 at 4pm, with stores and facilities opening in stages and the grand opening planned for 2027. The leisure offer includes cinemas (operator not named), go-kart tracks, children's play areas and arcade games, alongside three zones: De L'Avenue for fashion and luxury, The Valley for international dining and The Promenade, facing the waterfront. The release gives no GLA, store count or brand names and calls the mall the largest of its kind in the region, a claim it does not support with figures.

Why it matters: A leisure-led mall is opening in the Eastern Province alongside The Avenues Khobar and Westfield Dammam, and the directory still prints it as upcoming under a different spelling.

Arabian AlEsaar press release (ACCESS Newswire, via FinancialContent) · 29 Oct 2026 ↗A

Al Othaim Markets says the 2027 utility tariffs will add about 0.5% of revenue to its annual costs

DestinationsSaudi ArabiaFinancial29 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Abdullah Al Othaim Markets told Tadawul that the electricity, water and sewage tariff changes taking effect on 1 Jan 2027 will raise its annual operating costs by about 0.5% of consolidated revenue, with the effect showing from Q1 2027, and that it is cutting energy use and improving efficiency to offset the rise (Argaam, 29 Oct). SASCO told Tadawul the same day that its operating costs will rise from 1 Jan 2027, without a figure.

Why it matters: It is the first company figure on what the 2027 Saudi utility tariffs will cost a large retailer, a cost line mall owners and their tenants will also carry.

Argaam · 29 Oct 2026 ↗BArgaam (SASCO) · 29 Oct 2026 ↗B

Nahdi Medical now expects its 2026 profit to fall, citing geopolitical pressures

DestinationsSaudi ArabiaFinancial29 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Nahdi Medical's management said on its Q3 2026 earnings call that it has lowered its full-year 2026 net profit forecast and now expects a year-on-year decline, Argaam reported on 29 Oct, attributing the cut to ongoing geopolitical pressures. The size of the revision and the Q3 figures are behind Argaam's paywall and are not reported here.

Why it matters: A profit warning from one of Saudi Arabia's largest pharmacy chains is an early read on how the regional security situation is weighing on Saudi retail trading.

Argaam · 29 Oct 2026 ↗B

Eko Atlantic Mall is still to be built, and the developer publishes 107,500 sqm of net leasable area, not 120,000 sqm

LuLu signs for a 210,000 sq ft hypermarket in Maqam Holding's planned Al Shahama project, due by UAE National Day 2027

LuLu Group signed an agreement with Maqam International Holding for a 210,000 sq ft hypermarket in Al Shahama, Abu Dhabi, to be built by Maqam's SUNAD Real Estate Management as part of a larger housing and retail project Maqam plans there; Gulf News says it will open by UAE National Day 2027 and serve Al Shahama, Al Bahia and Yas Island. Home Depot will admit AI shopping agents that identify themselves and the customer they act for

AI & techGlobalAI / tech29 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Home Depot will support AI agents that search, compare and buy for customers, provided they identify themselves as agents and confirm whom they represent, so the retailer keeps contact with the shopper, chief information officer Angie Brown told Business Insider, as reported by PYMNTS on 29 Oct. The company is in talks with Meta on how its Muse agent should introduce itself; the draft Personal Agent Protocol from Meta and Sierra, which Walmart, Shopify and Stripe are helping develop, does not yet cover payments.

Why it matters: A second large US retailer is choosing identity rules over blocking, the choice GCC retailers, mall apps and banks face as agents such as Muse reach new markets.

PYMNTS (citing Business Insider) · 29 Oct 2026 ↗B
