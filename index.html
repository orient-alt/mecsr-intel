Torunlar GYO sets the final price for Forum Kayseri AVM at EUR 131,904,579

DestinationsTürkiye · KayseriDeal / JV16 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Add now

Torunlar GYO has set the final price for 100% of Forum Kayseri Gayrimenkul Yatırım A.Ş., the company that owns and operates Forum Kayseri AVM, at EUR 131,904,579 after financial due diligence, against EUR 132,230,877.34 announced at first. The seller was Germany-based Union Investment Real Estate GmbH: the share purchase agreement was signed on 30 January 2026, the transfer completed on 2 February 2026 and the price was paid in cash. The update was filed on KAP on 12 October and reported by Mall Report on 16 October; neither gives the mall's GLA or store count, and Torunlar's H1 2026 activity report also records the purchase of Forum Mersin.

Why it matters: It confirms a new owner for a regional Turkish mall that the Directory does not yet list, and adds a disclosed transaction value to Türkiye's retail investment record.

Abu Dhabi sets up TAMM Global to license its AI-powered government services platform abroad

CXUAE · Abu DhabiLaunch16 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Article idea

The Department of Government Enablement, Abu Dhabi announced TAMM Global on 16 October 2026, under a directive from Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed, Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi. The company will develop and manage the TAMM platform, hold its intellectual property and license its architecture and implementation methods to governments and organisations, which can adapt prebuilt services to their own legal frameworks. The National and Gulf News report more than 1,200 public and private-sector services, customer satisfaction of 94.5% (78% in 2022, before AI was integrated), service costs down 30% and about nine fewer service-centre visits per user a year.

Why it matters: A Gulf government is now selling its customer-experience model as a product, which gives MECSC a citable satisfaction benchmark and a strong speaker lead for RCM Jeddah.

Amazon and eBay block AI shopping agents as agent traffic climbs ahead of the holiday season

AI & techGlobalMarket data16 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Semafor reported on 16 October 2026 that Amazon and eBay have blocked AI shopping agents to protect their advertising businesses and checkout experiences, as online retailers prepare for the holiday season. Cheq, a cybersecurity company that verifies AI shopping agents, told Semafor of a 100x surge in agentic traffic over a 45-day period after new consumer AI agents were released; this is a vendor figure and its method was not given. Morning Consult research cited in the piece finds shoppers about as likely to be swayed by an agent's recommendation as by an influencer's, though few are comfortable letting AI buy for them.

Why it matters: Gulf retailers and mall e-commerce teams face the same choice between blocking and courting AI agents that global marketplaces are now making.

Semafor · 16 Oct 2026 ↗B

Apparel Group's AIVI app, the successor to 6thStreet, adds an AI stylist and virtual try-on

AI & techGCCLaunch8 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: Medium

Apparel Group launched AIVI in August 2026 as 'the evolution of 6thStreet', one platform for its brands with Club AIVI rewards, citing more than 2,600 stores and over 85 international brands in 14 countries. Gulf News (updated 8 October 2026) describes an 'Ask AIVI' adviser that takes spoken or typed requests and suggests items from preferences, past searches and purchases, a virtual try-on from a photo or selfie, and Club AIVI, the renamed Club Apparel, which works across online platforms, stores and food and beverage outlets. The app is available in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman, with selected products delivered in as little as 60 minutes in the UAE.

Why it matters: One of the largest fashion tenants in Gulf malls now runs discovery, try-on and loyalty through a conversational AI app that links online and in-mall stores.

Gulf News · 8 Oct 2026 ↗BApparel Group (press release) · 3 Aug 2026 ↗A

WEF and Bain: AI could unlock more than $1 trillion of economic and sustainability value in MENA by 2030

AI & techMENA · DubaiMarket data14 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: High

The World Economic Forum published 'Unlocking Value: AI for Resilient Growth in MENA', developed with Bain & Company, on 14 October 2026 in Dubai, alongside a data-centre infrastructure report produced with SRMG. The estimate of more than $1 trillion by 2030 is made up of up to $200 billion of sustainable financing, $370 billion of public-sector-enabled sustainable investment, $250 billion of long-term asset losses avoided, $90 billion of higher energy-sector valuations and $100 billion of cross-sector projects. In a survey of the MENA Chief Sustainability Officers Network, 89% saw high or very high potential for AI, but only 40 to 55% said their use cases were at the pilot or scaled stage; the release gives no retail or consumer figures.

Why it matters: It is a citable regional AI headline for MECSR content, provided it is quoted with its scope: finance, energy, the public sector and sustainability, not retail.
