Big US banks restrict third-party AI agents, and JPMorgan bars them from moving money

AI & techGlobalAI / tech23 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

Tom's Hardware, relaying an Axios report, said on 23 Oct that major US banks have detected AI agents accessing customer accounts and in some cases attempting transactions, and that Morgan Stanley, Bank of America, JPMorgan and Wells Fargo are blocking the technology and changing their terms of service, with some moving to read-only access. JPMorgan already lets outside agents such as Meta's Muse view some account data read-only but not send money or pay bills; Jamie Dimon told analysts on 13 Oct that the bank would not allow 'just any outside agent' in.

Why it matters: This is the banking side of the split over shopping agents (Amazon and eBay block them, Walmart admits them): Gulf banks and payment networks face the same choice, and it will decide whether shoppers' AI agents can pay at Gulf retailers.

Tom's Hardware (relaying Axios) · 23 Oct 2026 ↗BCybernews · 14 Oct 2026 ↗B

Open Jais 3 Arabic models include Jais Speech, which transcribes dialects from 19 Arab countries

AI & techUAE · Abu DhabiLaunch15 Oct 2026 · recentConfidence: High

MBZUAI's Institute of Foundation Models and Abu Dhabi's Inception42 have released Jais 3, a family of open Arabic language models, with Jais Speech, which transcribes Modern Standard Arabic and dialects spoken in 19 Arab countries, keeps English or French words in their original script when speakers switch language, and identifies the dialect. The developers say the largest model leads open models on agentic tasks and Jais Speech had the lowest average word and character error rates of the models compared, and they are publishing a suite of 24 Arabic benchmarks. Gulf News reported the launch on 15 Oct and The National followed on 24 Oct.

Why it matters: Open, dialect-aware Arabic speech and agent models could cut the cost of Arabic voice bots and contact-centre AI for Gulf retailers, malls and service centres.

Gulf News · 15 Oct 2026 ↗BThe National · 24 Oct 2026 ↗B
