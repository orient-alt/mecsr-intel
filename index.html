Egypt's central bank sets rules for banks' use of agents, including complaint notices and fee disclosure

CXEgypt · CairoPolicy9 Nov 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium

The Central Bank of Egypt has approved rules on how banks may use banking agents, which, once approved by the CBE, can identify and verify customers and update their information on a bank's behalf. Agency contracts may run for no more than three years, with the CBE told at least three months before expiry. Agents must display how customers can complain at their premises, state the fees and commissions charged and the days and hours services are available, and pass documents to the bank within 30 working days. Eligible agents include non-bank institutions supervised by the Financial Regulatory Authority, government bodies and Egyptian missions abroad, and the CBE presents the rules as part of its financial inclusion drive.

Why it matters: Egypt now sets service duties, including complaint notices and fee and hours disclosure, for third parties that serve bank customers, a reference point for customer-service standards in Egyptian retail and banking.

Daily News Egypt (via Zawya) · 9 Nov 2026 ↗B
