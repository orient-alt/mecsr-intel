Talaat Moustafa Group and the Egyptian state agree four mixed-use mega-projects worth about EGP 11 trillion

DestinationsEgypt · CairoPipeline20 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Watch

Talaat Moustafa Group (TMG) reached an agreement with the Egyptian state on 20 Oct 2026, announced at Egypt Forum 2026 in the New Administrative Capital, for four mixed-use projects with total targeted investment of about EGP 11 trillion.

Daily News Egypt lists an integrated destination of about 5 million sqm on the strip linking Suez Road to Madinaty in East Cairo, with commercial, administrative, recreational and residential uses (EGP 3.2 trillion); an 11.3 million sqm urban project in Alexandria (EGP 1.7 trillion); a 10 million sqm industrial, logistics, real estate and tourism development beside the new Abu Qir port (EGP 5 trillion); and a 10.4 million sqm North Coast destination modelled on SouthMED (EGP 970 billion). No retail areas or dates were given.

Why it matters: TMG, the developer behind Madinaty and Al Rehab, is lining up schemes whose commercial parts will add to future mall and retail supply in Greater Cairo, Alexandria and the North Coast.

HDP TULUA launches Navy 26, a commercial scheme with retail and a hypermarket in 6th of October City

DestinationsEgypt · 6th of October CityNew property20 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: Medium→ 2027 Directory: Check first

HDP TULUA Developments has launched Navy 26, a commercial and services project on 26 feddans on the Boulevard axis of 6th of October City, West Cairo, Zawya reported on 20 Oct 2026. The investment is given as EGP 5 billion in the text (over $92mln in the headline), with a building footprint of 12,000 sqm, about 10% of the site.

Planned uses include retail outlets, a hypermarket, restaurants and cafés, entertainment, hospitality, a medical complex, offices and banking services. Completion is due within three and a half years, and expressions of interest opened on 18 Oct.

Why it matters: A new West Cairo destination with a hypermarket, F&B and entertainment, and a possible new Egypt listing for the 2027 Directory.

Arada launches Arada KSA with a SAR50bn land bank and a first SAR14bn master plan in Riyadh

DestinationsSaudi Arabia · RiyadhMarket entry20 Oct 2026 · in windowConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Watch

UAE developer Arada launched Arada KSA on 20 Oct 2026, backed by a land bank of about 10 million square metres in Riyadh, Jeddah and Makkah that it values at SAR50 billion. Its first project is a SAR14 billion residential master plan on a plot of about 2.5 million square metres in Al Rimal District, north-eastern Riyadh, with an estimated 3,500 villas and townhouses.

Planned amenities include retail, a community centre, a school and sports facilities; no mall is named. Construction and sales are due to start in 2027.

Why it matters: A major UAE community developer is bringing new neighbourhood retail to Riyadh's north-east growth corridor and holds land in Jeddah and Makkah, which matters for the 2027 Directory pipeline and for RCM Jeddah.

Majid Al Futtaim's Precision Media launches Precision Insights, an AI platform that links in-store and online ads to sales

AI & techUAE · DubaiLaunch19 Oct 2026 · recentMECSR memberConfidence: High→ 2027 Directory: Article idea

Precision Media, the retail media business of Majid Al Futtaim Retail, launched Precision Insights, an AI-powered platform that measures campaign performance across stores and online channels, at its first Retail Media Summit at the Museum of the Future in Dubai on 19 Oct 2026. It also presented viewed-impression measurement and billing, AI audiences built from more than 50 consumer journey signals, and shoppable ads linked to the Carrefour online catalogue with closed-loop attribution.

The release cites more than 420 Carrefour and HyperMax stores, 600 million store visitors a year, 320 million digital visits and more than 14 million SHARE members across ten markets.

Why it matters: Retail media that proves sales turns store and mall traffic into a second revenue line for retailers and landlords.
